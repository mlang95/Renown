# Renown — World Lore Framework

Companion reference to the mechanics/build docs. **Governing rule: lore derives from mechanics, never invented alongside.**

Fifteen cultures = 4 heartlands + 6 pairs + 4 triples (Polymaths) + 1 Generalist, mapped to geographic rings.

---

## Core Spine — the setting's first principle

- **Terrain identity is fixed** (the simplex / domain-substrate); **every name is a narrator's claim** (perspectival). Applied across world-vs-sea naming, sea sub-names, place exonym/endonym, and population-vs-terrain.
- **Population is a demographic overlay** on fixed terrain — people displace and migrate across regions; a region's terrain-domain ≠ who currently lives there. This lets anyone realistically start anywhere and go anywhere (medieval-hard travel, but routes exist).
- **Map domain-points are tendencies, not hard demarcations** — a region is where a domain's cultures *tend* to cluster. Gradient, not discrete.
- **Names carry register-tone** signalling the speaker's relationship to a place (endonym/exonym, pride/contempt, or translation — e.g. *Lost Woods* ≈ *Lenaveron*, "woods of the lost").

## Naming Grammar — the org-form made personal

| Domain | Grammar | Example | Affiliation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Prowess** | `[name] of the [Band]` | Dreg of the Clenched Fist | **Announced** — loud, a banner. Band nouns = weapons (Fist / Host / Blade). |
| **Cunning** | `[name] [earned byname]` | Sliv Three-Knife | **Concealed** — brotherhood never stated; known by deed. Byname is *given* by others (method / trait / deed). |
| **Industry** | `[name] [craft-surname] [numeral]` | Frank Smith IV | Lineage + inherited trade. |
| **Piety** | `St. [name] the [Order-adj]` | St. Paul the Brannochite | **Assumed** — born name *erased* on joining; you take a saint-name (a reused office, not a person). |

**Named-figure place formats:**
- `St. [name]` → holy site (Piety)
- `[Terrain] of [name]` → honored hero (Prowess — e.g. Glen of Pravak)
- `[name]'s [grim object]` → feared strongman (Cunning×Prowess — e.g. Vogen's Gallows)

## Reputation Matrix *(relational — each domain viewed through the viewer's own value)*

Diagonal = self-image. Matrix is **asymmetric** (A-of-B ≠ B-of-A) — that asymmetry *is* the inter-domain politics.

| Viewer ↓ judges → | **Prowess** | **Cunning** | **Industry** | **Piety** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Prowess** *(by strength)* | *the brave* | cowards / snakes | soft / merchants | zealots (worthy or heathen) |
| **Cunning** *(by leverage)* | blunt tools | *the clever* | cheats / parasites | fools to exploit |
| **Industry** *(by productivity)* | wasteful destroyers | marks to fleece | *the builders* | worldly / greedy |
| **Piety** *(by faith)* | godly warriors **or** savage heathens | deceivers / the false — **heretic**, special venom | fanatics / bad-for-business | *the saved* |

Backfills "defined against" and "historical relationship to neighbors." **Sharpest cell = Piety↔Cunning** (orthodox vs. heretic of the same god).

## The Sacred — each domain deifies its own activity

The four "gods" are **not parallel deities.** Each domain deifies its own core activity/value; **only Piety worships a literal god.**

- **Prowess — War is their god.** The fight *is* worship; glory-or-death is the sacrament. A warrior-cult (Norse / einherjar flavor), not petitioning a being.
- **Cunning — the game/process itself.** Addicted to the play; sabotage, theft, deception, blackmail are the sacraments of a way of life. The **False Prophet is a tool they weaponize** (faith as the ultimate long con), *not* worshipped — Cunning has no real god of its own, so it hijacks Piety's.
- **Industry — the work.** Building / order / Peace deified, or atheist.
- **Piety — the God of Gods.** The one literal, transcendent deity.
- **Center — influence / proximity to power** (see Center section).

**Oppositions:**
- **God of War (Prowess) ↔ God of Peace (Industry)** — *War-led vs. Peace-led* is the fork that flips a Prowess×Industry culture's whole archetype (conqueror vs. arms-trader).
- **God of Gods (Piety) ↔ False Prophet (Cunning)** — *not* opposed faiths but **orthodox vs. heretic of the same god.** The False Prophet is a corrupt schism (Mormon-split / prosperity-cult analog): same god, leadership extorts followers. Belief *exploited*, not believed. *Prophet's Landing* = this false church.

**Believers vs. doubters:** Prowess & Piety are true believers (locked). Cunning & Industry are doubters (may be atheist).

## Piety Structure — papacy & schism

- **Piety = a papacy** — a single supreme vessel (pope-analog), *chosen* not born. Fits "chosen vessel" succession and name-erasure: the office continues, the individual dissolves.
- **False Prophet = an antipope** — a rival false seat. A schism is sharpest when there is *one* true seat to break from (Western Schism model).
- **Militant holy-orders** (warrior-nun / Templar / crusade — Sisters-of-Battle-adjacent as *confirmation, not template*) concentrate in **Piety×Prowess**, *not* pure Piety. Pure Piety stays devotional / monastic.
- **Aesthetic left open** (Tier 3) — the God of Gods can be enthroned / Gothic, but don't overbuild on one inspiration.
- **Schism discipline:** Piety is the most fracturable domain (many split-offs planned). Each schism names the *one* axis it broke over (who holds the seat / how you reach the god / quiet vs. armed / merciful vs. wrathful), inheriting ~90% of pure Piety — blend logic applied *within* a domain.

## The Ages — world-historical delivery layer

- The Ages **do not cycle.** They are the **total story** of the world — linear, one arc, beginning to end. Each Age is a chapter / overture.
- An Age is tied to a **domain's dominance** over that timeframe (that domain sets the conditions everyone lives under) — but the Age is **not that domain's story.** Everyone's story is told in every Age; the dominant domain just sets the terms. (A Prowess-dominant Age = war sets the terms and *all* cultures navigate it — not "the Prowess chapter.")
- **Ages are overtures:** short tonal pieces that surface *one* undercurrent as lived experience — the dominance/tension of that age's dominant motif — **without explaining the machinery.** All the framework logic (reputation matrix, sacred-as-activity, the simplex) stays **undercurrent**: invisible, felt not read. Grounded logic underneath, non-overt tone on top.
- **Age of Influence = the fifth and final Age** (5 Ages total). It closes the arc and reframes the world's **end state**: everything resolving into influence / positioning — the center's proximity-worship as the world's destination (the hollow center's triumph, a cynical endpoint). Echoes the 4-corners-plus-center geometry as *time* (4 Ages + 1 final center Age).

> **Eras are unrelated to lore.** An Era is an *empire's* lifecycle timeline (a mechanics axis) — do not conflate Ages (world-story) with Eras (empire lifecycle).

## The Center / Generalist-quad (Vaelohk) Culture

- **Sacred = influence / proximity to power.** They worship being close to the Duchy — access, status by nearness. Not a domain-value (war / game / work / god) but **position.** Completes the sacred pattern: four corners deify an *activity*, the center deifies *proximity-to-power* — the most cynical, powerful purely by being near the powerful, producing nothing of its own.
- **Core trait = status-vanity** (vain for being close to the Duchy). Distinct from Industry's *greed*-vanity: the center is *status/proximity* vanity.
- **Culture = court / courtiers** (Versailles / Byzantine-court flavor — intrigue, flattery, positioning). Grounded, educated tone — home to the most education (generalists) and the Duke.
- **Thematic point:** the corners have convictions; the center has only access. The center plays the actual game mechanics (influence / standings / alliances) most nakedly — it worships what *Renown* is.
- **People = the Astravantheliad** — the one demonym rendered hyper-intellectual, using all four consonant families; the erudite, pretentious name performs their vanity. (Place-names on the island stay plain common-tongue — The Duchy's Mouth, Bay of Renown.)

## Blend Rule *(pairs / triples)*

- Blends **don't average** — you **pick which parent leads** each axis; the **god often decides the fork.** Two cultures of the same domain-set differ by rolling different leads. This is the "anyone anywhere" release valve at the culture level.
- **Diagonal pairs** (non-adjacent corners, no shared border) = **Prowess×Cunning** and **Piety×Industry** → enclaves / islands.
- **OPEN TENSION:** the god-oppositions (War↔Peace = Prowess↔Industry; God-of-Gods↔False-Prophet = Piety↔Cunning) are *not* the geographic diagonals. So either the theological-opposition pairs are geographically *adjacent* (they border and clash directly — arguably stronger: enemies are neighbors), or the oppositions need rethinking. Geography and religion no longer auto-reinforce — **to resolve.**

## Per-Domain Lore — Voice & Medium

| Domain | Voice (register) | Medium (how it survives) |
|---|---|---|
| **Prowess** | Armies, fighting, captures, sacks, tribute | Written historical **analysis** — leadership, army management, logistics, strategy, tactics, campaigns (scholarly military history) |
| **Cunning** | Denial ("it never happened") **or** victim mindset — never a straight confession; the telling is itself a con | **Folklore & mystery** — no records, only rumor/legend, deniable by nature |
| **Industry** | Centuries refining the family's sacred inherited craft, known only because the bloodline passes it down | Family **oral tradition** + statues, monuments, epitaphs (the objects outlive the tellers) |
| **Piety** | "Just insane shit" — unhinged, fervent, beyond rational register | **Epistles & administrative documents** — church bureaucracy; the mania in dry official form is the horror |

*The medium reinforces the domain:* Prowess documents its glory; Cunning leaves nothing pinnable (the point); Industry's legacy is touchable; Piety delivers insanity in bureaucratic clothing.

## Regional Lore

- **The Lost Woods** (E, Piety×Prowess) — sacred to the crusader order; the pious travel there to "meet the God of Gods." **The reality:** the crusaders in the woods cut the pilgrims down — the "meeting" is death. The order is *both* protector (against the northern Prowess "barbarians") *and* slaughterer of its own pilgrims — piety-unto-death is the point. **Overtly, suspiciously devout** — devotion so extreme it curdles; the performance of holiness is the tell that something is wrong.
- **The Wheat Fields** (E, on the Lost Woods seam) — tongue-in-cheek name for the *perpetual battlefield* where crusaders defend the Lost Woods from "the barbarians of the north." Never paved or pathed — it is hallowed ground. (Reaping double-meaning: men cut down like wheat.)
- **Tombs of the Old Gods** (far SE) — site of the **Great Basilica**, an old-wonder; a great pious civilization existed here, now gone.
- **Draggath Wastes** (NE, Prowess) — *was not always* arid badlands. Once the seat of great kingdoms — the **Great Monarchy** that ruled the whole continent. A past **Great Fractioning** shattered it into city-states and mini-dictatorships → a massive civil war of many small factions (Greek city-state analog), ruining the landscape, warring over the ruins of prior civilizations. This forged the **warrior-caste** system over time. *Draggath is named for the final monarch who held the continent whole* — now a nomadic warband culture with some static city-states, perpetually training for inevitable, perpetual conflict.
- **Prophet's Landing** (central-S, Cunning×Piety) — a beautiful Caribbean-esque tropical archipelago where Cunning×Piety cultures live to **extort the false religion of the False Prophet.** Paradise as bait — the con dressed as heaven.
- **Vaelohk** (center) — very grounded in tone; home to the most education (the generalists) and the Duke. The learned, grounded heart.

## Named Historical Events

**The Pincer of Dead Waters** *(Prowess tactical feat — names Dead Waters Bay).* Every northern assault on the Wheat Fields had broken on the Lost Woods treeline — attackers pinned against the woods with the sea at their back. A Prowess warlord took the *minimum* slice of Industry to raise a **single, disposable shipyard** on the then-unnamed northern bay (demonstrating hybrid domain-point spending — a sliver of a second domain for one unlock, not becoming an Industry power). The double-move: the main host drove south from the north — the expected assault, drawing the defenders up to the treeline — *while* the shipyard-born flotilla landed on their flank and rear from the water. The defenders folded. It became **the most successful assault on the Lost Woods to date**, and the bay was named **Dead Waters** for what came out of it. *Perspectival split:* "most successful assault" is the attacker's framing; the Lost Woods crusaders name the day a massacre / a drowning. Proves Prowess is **tactical**, not merely brute. Fits a Prowess-dominant Age. *(Hero name = placeholder, not yet locked.)*

## Phonology *(governs constructed names; plain-English descriptor names bypass it)*

**Consonant classes:**

| Domain | Consonants | Signature |
|---|---|---|
| Prowess | voiced stops + gutturals — g, k, b, d, r, kh, gh | blunt, hammered |
| Cunning | sibilants + affricates — s, sh, z, ts, x | hissing; the s→hard-stop move (Vraskil, Skorgath) sounds like a blade drawn |
| Industry | voiceless stops + clusters — t, p, tr, st, kt, pr, ft | clipped, mechanical |
| Piety | liquids + nasals + soft fricatives — l, m, n, th, v, soft-s | flowing, sung |

- **Vowel harmony = purity:** pure corner = one vowel class; pair = two; triple = three (harmony breaks); center = neutral (a, ə), open — but the *center demonym* deliberately shatters harmony (all classes) to signal it commands all domains.
- **Ending:** open (vowel-final / soft -n) = center / loose; closed (hard stop) = corner / sealed. Distance-from-corner is readable in how clipped vs. open the ending is.
- **Collision fix:** keep Prowess on *voiced* stops (g, b, d) and Industry on *voiceless* clusters (tr, st, pr) so their velars don't sound identical.
- **Established root family:** Prowess war-corner = **Dra- / Drag-** (Drakenheart, Sea of Drakes, Draggath, Bluffs of Vorghast) — one culture's tongue stamped across land, sea, and settlement.

## The Map (Vaelohk) — current draft

- **World name = Vaelohk = the center island** (shared). The center holds the world-name; the sea stays contested and locally-named. The center is the Duke's seat / Generalist-quad, named in plain common-tongue (The Duchy's Mouth, Bay of Renown).

**Corners:**
- **Industry** — NW (Blighthold, castle in mountains; Scarlet Forest)
- **Prowess** — NE (Draggath Wastes [tundra], Bleak Highlands, Glen of Pravak, Drakenheart, Sea of Drakes)
- **Cunning** — SW (Dreadwood, Marrow Shoals)
- **Piety** — S/SE (Lenaveron core + Lost Woods)

**The sea** — named perspectivally (Gulf-of-Mexico logic): *Lonely Sea / Sea of Faith / Sea of Damnation* = the same water from different shores. No master sea name.

**Pairs:**
- Piety×Industry → White Forest / Fair Whitewood (+ Lemstet Bay)
- Prowess×Industry → Port of War / Drakenheart
- Cunning×Industry → Bay of Pigs / Marrow Shoals *(Bay of Pigs = weaponized false-exonym; locals keep the dismissive name to lure merchant prey)*
- Piety×Prowess → Wharf of St. Brannoch
- Prowess×Cunning → Dark Isle / Vogen's Gallows (enclave)
- Piety×Cunning → Shallow Mire / Quiet Hollow (marsh)
- Industry×Cunning → Crag Pass (chokepoint)

**Triples:**
- Piety×Prowess×Cunning → Cailendroff Isles
- Cunning×Industry×Prowess → Isle of Trade area
- Cunning×Piety×Industry → Prophet's Landing
- Piety×Industry×Prowess → *unplaced* (Lost Woods northern end)

**Twelfth Reach** — literally 12 islands; a diaspora zone receiving displaced Prowess×Industry from Port of War / Drakenheart.

**Naming registers (3 families + center):**
1. Phonological / constructed — Vaelohk, Cailendroff, Lenaveron, Vorghast
2. Overt-grim — Dreadwood, Sea of Damnation
3. Understated-grim — Bay of Pigs, Wheat Fields, Quiet Hollow, Flat Pass *(mild names hiding violence)*
4. Center — plain common-tongue

**Draggath founding-history:** it was fertile once; endless Prowess warfare (deforestation, overgrazing, salted earth) turned it to arid badlands — the war-culture consumed its own prize, and the ruins *are* the former bounty. Real model: Mesopotamia / the Fertile Crescent.

## Map Production (Wonderdraft) — practical notes

- Current draft is **1920×1080 (16:9)** — too low-res and wrong ratio for print. Wonderdraft locks resolution at creation and cannot upscale.
- **Plan:** iterate design at 1920 (cheap; layout transfers), then do **one trace-rebuild at high res** when the design is locked (import the old PNG as a trace image, re-paint terrain over it). Upscale trigger = when you shift from *deciding* to *polishing*.
- **Target: 3:2 aspect ratio** (suits the wide ringed-sea, prints well, ≈ a US-Letter two-page spread at 1.55:1). Reframe 16:9 → 3:2 by **adding height, not cropping width** (the edges hold content). Rebuild at ~**7200×4800** (24×36" @ 200 dpi). Keep **Vaelohk off the vertical centerline** (the spine/gutter would bisect it); add bleed.
- **Labels:** stacked / gently-rotated with heavy letter-spacing for ranges (Rockies-map style); horizontal + leader line for features you can't cover (Crag Pass); never cover terrain; halo/glow keeps text readable over busy background.
- **Climate:** the cold-arid gradient (tundra → badlands) needs an intermediary **cold-steppe** band — white → grey-green → green-brown (grassland peak) → tan → red. Mountains band by **altitude** (snow → rock → forest → lowland), a *different* gradient. The sea is bigger-than-Mediterranean / smaller-than-ocean, so the climate variety is defensible. Wonderdraft has **no elevation tool** — rivers are faked by hand (start in mountains, only merge downstream, one outlet, never cross ranges).
