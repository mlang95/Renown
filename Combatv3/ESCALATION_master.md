# RENOWN — ESCALATION CAMPAIGN
*A fast, battle-driven mode for 2+ players. Ten turns.*

## DICE PRINCIPLE
Every roll works the same way. A target is written **X+**: roll that number or higher on a **D6** to succeed — a **lower** target is easier (a Sergeant's 2+ to Strike beats a Levy's 4+). Apply modifiers to the **roll**: **+1** per bonus, **−1** per penalty. **+** always helps you, **−** always hurts: each negative modifier requires your roll to be one higher to succeed, each positive modifier one lower. **If a 7 or more is ever needed to succeed a roll, it automatically fails; if a 1 or less is ever needed, it automatically passes.**

A **natural** roll is the die before modifiers. A **natural 6** triggers **Cleave, Deadly, Destroy Shield and Riposte** (and a natural-6 Save fails against Poison). **Note:** if a roll automatically passes or fails but a natural result could still trigger an effect, you still roll the die to see whether that effect triggers.

**The rolls:** **Roll-off** — higher Seizes the Initiative; **To Strike** — roll ≥ to-Strike number; **Save** — roll + AP + shield bonus ≥ armor value; **Recover** — roll ≥ N; **Parry** — roll ≥ 5 (Improved 4+), natural 6 = Riposte; **Morale** — roll ≥ Morale value.

## SETUP
- Each player starts with **25 Levy retinues, Farm Tools, Cloth armor, no shield**.
- All four Domains (**Industry, Prowess, Piety, Cunning**) start **Untested** (no effect).
- No Buildings, 0 Victory Points (VP).
- **Every Battle is fought at an army size of 25.** What carries over between battles is your **standings, buildings, unlocks and VP**.

## TURN SEQUENCE
**1 — BATTLE.** Pair off and fight one Battle (rotate pairings each turn).
- **Destroy the enemy army completely (wipe out every retinue): +3 VP.**
- **Win the Battle: +2 VP.**
- **Lose, but end it via a successful Fall Back with 1+ retinue remaining: +1 VP.**
- Lose any other way (wiped out, Routed, failed Fall Back): 0 VP.

**2 — ADVANCEMENT** (all players simultaneously):
- **Unlock one Domain Standing** (optional): advance any one Domain one step — Untested → Rising → Established → Sovereign.
- **Perform one Build Action**: construct one Building whose Standing you hold.

## EDICTS (+1 VP each)
Gain **+1 VP** every time you reach a **Sovereign** Standing in any Domain, or **complete (master) a Monument**: *Advanced Blast Furnace, Royal Pavilion, Preceptory of the Knight's Templar, Ministry of Military Strategy, Outrider Intercept Post.*

## BUILDING
A Build Action constructs **one Building**; the only requirement is its **Build Action Requirement** (a Domain Standing — no prerequisite Buildings). Its **Innate** effect applies at once; its **Mastery** effect once every Building in its **Mastery Requirement** is also built. You may freely re-equip before each Battle with anything unlocked.

## BATTLE WALKTHROUGH
**Begin the Battle — roll off for the Initiative.** Each player rolls one D6 — **+1 if you won your last Battle** — re-rolling ties. The higher roller **Seizes the Initiative**: they are the **Attacker** for this Battle and gain **+1 Initiative in the first Skirmish**; their opponent is the **Defender**.

Fought as a series of **Skirmishes** — run these steps each Skirmish until the Battle ends; rule detail is in the Glossary.

1. **Form the line.** Each side puts up to **10** retinues in the front line — one attack each — and may keep up to 5 in reserve to replace losses.
2. **Choose Tactics.** Both players secretly pick one Tactic, then reveal together.
3. **Declare equipment.** The Attacker names their equipment first; the Defender then names theirs.
4. **Initiative.** Initiative runs from **−2 to +2** (the Ministry monument can raise the maximum to +3). The higher Initiative Strikes first this Skirmish. At **−2 or lower you Blunder** — you can only Strike on a **natural 6**.
5. **Roll to Strike.** Roll a D6 for each front-line retinue (+/− its modifiers). It Strikes on a result **≥ its to-Strike number** (Levy 4+, Man-at-Arms 3+, Sergeant 2+, Knight Templar 3+). A natural 6 may trigger Cleave, Deadly or Destroy Shield.
6. **Strike and defend.** Resolve the first side's Strikes — Parry, then Save, then Recover; the rest are casualties. Casualties leave the field at once, lowering its Field count.
7. **Panic check.** If the side about to Strike back took **5 or more casualties** this Skirmish, it takes a **Panic check** first: roll its Morale, **up to 5 dice** (failures are casualties). If the check is ever **modified to 7 or more, that army Routs**.
8. **Strike back.** The other side Strikes the same way, if able, and takes any Panic check triggered inside it (5 or more casualties).
9. **Lose Endurance.** Each side that fought loses 1 Endurance. A side at 0 Endurance is **Fatigued**.
10. **Break check.** Each **Fatigued** side's field takes a **break check** — before it gains a token: roll a D6 per retinue in the field, **up to 5 dice**, each **≥ its modified Morale value** (Levy 5+, MaA 4+, Sgt 4+, KT 3+). Failures are casualties; a break check never triggers a Panic check. A check ever **modified to 7 or more Routs the whole army**.
11. **Fatigue token.** Then each Fatigued side gains a **Fatigue token** — each is **−1 to its Strike and Morale rolls**, and they stack.
12. **End the Skirmish.** The Battle ends if a side is wiped out, Routs, or successfully **Falls Back**. Otherwise start the next Skirmish at step 1.

## TACTIC MATRIX
*Both players pick a Tactic in secret each Skirmish (Battle step 2), then reveal together. Find **your** Tactic in the left column and your **opponent's** across the top — the cell shows the modifiers **you** gain that Skirmish (your opponent reads their own row).*

***I** = Initiative · **H** = to-Strike (easier to hit) · **S** = Save (harder to be killed). + helps you, − hurts you. **Battle ends** = the fight breaks off this Skirmish; **No combat** = both Flank (no engagement, Endurance still spent).*

| You ↓ / Foe → | Scout | Ambush | Flank | Charge | Fighting Formation | Defensive Formation | Fall Back |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Scout** | +1 I | +1 I | −1 I | +1 I | +1 I | +1 I | Battle ends |
| **Ambush** | −1 I +1 S | −1 I +1 S | +1 I −1 H | +1 I +1 H | +1 I +1 S | −1 H | Battle ends |
| **Flank** | +1 I | −1 I +1 H | No combat | −1 I | +1 I +1 H | +1 I | −1 I |
| **Charge** | −1 I | −1 I −1 S | +1 I | +1 H | +1 I −1 H | −1 I −1 S | +1 I +1 H |
| **Fighting Formation** | −1 I +1 H | −1 I −1 S | −1 I | −1 I +1 H | −1 I +1 H | +1 H | +1 H |
| **Defensive Formation** | −1 I +1 S | +1 S | −1 I +1 S | +1 I +1 H +1 S | +1 S | +1 S | Battle ends |

## GLOSSARY
- **2H** — Two-handed: no shield. A Bastard Sword fights one-handed with a shield until the shield breaks, then uses its 2H stats.
- **AP** — Armor Penetration — a weapon's (negative) modifier to the defender's Save roll; the more negative, the harder to save.
- **Attacker / Defender** — Set by the roll-off. Each Skirmish the Attacker declares equipment first; the Defender then responds.
- **Battle** — One fight between two players (Turn step 1), resolved as a series of Skirmishes until a side is wiped out, Routs, or Falls Back.
- **Blocked** — −1 Initiative in the first Skirmish (negated by Immune Blocked).
- **Break check** — Taken by each Fatigued side's field every Skirmish, just before it gains its Fatigue token. Roll Morale (up to 5 dice); failures are casualties, but a break check never triggers a Panic check. Unbreakable retinues auto-hold.
- **Casualty** — A retinue removed from the field — from an unsaved Strike or a failed Panic or Break check.
- **Cleave** — Each natural 6 to Strike adds one extra Strike.
- **Deflect** — Every ranged weapon has it: a ranged Strike is −1 to Parry and never Ripostes.
- **Destroy Shield** — A natural 6 against a shield-bearer destroys their shield for the rest of the Battle.
- **Drilled** — No Endurance loss in the first Skirmish.
- **Dual-equip** — Carry two weapons at once (e.g. melee + ranged). Granted by the Tiltyard, which also gives Unwieldy until its mastery removes it.
- **Edict** — A scoring achievement worth +1 VP: reach a Sovereign Standing, or complete (master) a Monument.
- **Endurance** — A side's stamina. Each side that fights loses 1 per Skirmish; at 0 it becomes Fatigued.
- **Fall Back** — A controlled retreat that ends the Battle with at least one retinue left — a partial success worth +1 VP.
- **Fatigue token** — Each token is −1 to that army's Strike rolls and Morale rolls for the rest of the Battle. They stack.
- **Fatigued** — A side at 0 Endurance. It cannot Parry, Riposte or Recover; each Skirmish its field takes a break check, then it gains a Fatigue token.
- **Field** — Your retinues in play — front line (up to 10) plus reserve (up to 5). Casualties leave the field at once, lowering its count.
- **Heal X** — At the end of each Skirmish, for every X casualties you took from Strikes, return 1 retinue to your Army.
- **Immune [keyword]** — Cancels that keyword as it applies to you — e.g. Immune Unwieldy, Strain, Blocked, Panic or Destroy Shield.
- **Improved Parry** — Your Parry succeeds on 4+ instead of 5+.
- **Initiative** — Decides who Strikes first each Skirmish (higher first). Runs −2 to +2 (Ministry can raise the maximum to +3). At −2 or lower you Blunder — you can only Strike on a natural 6.
- **Monument** — A Domain's capstone Building (the purple rows). Completing one scores its Edict (+1 VP) and grants a powerful effect.
- **Morale** — How steady a retinue is when tested (Levy 5+, MaA 4+, Sgt 4+, KT 3+; lower is steadier). Break and Panic checks both roll it: a D6 per retinue in the field, up to 5 dice, each ≥ its modified Morale value; failures are casualties. Any such check ever modified to 7 or more Routs the whole army.
- **Natural roll** — The number on the die before any modifiers. A natural 6 triggers Cleave, Deadly, Destroy Shield and Riposte — modifiers never change what counts as 'natural'.
- **Nimble** — +1 Initiative in the first Skirmish.
- **One Shot** — A ranged weapon fires in the first Skirmish only.
- **Panic check** — Taken at once by a side that suffered 5 or more casualties in a Skirmish, before it Strikes back. Roll Morale (up to 5 dice); Immune Panic auto-passes it.
- **Parry** — Roll a D6 to cancel a Strike on a 5+ (a natural 6 is a Riposte). −1 versus Unstoppable and versus ranged. Cannot Parry while Fatigued.
- **Planishing** — Reduces an incoming Strike's AP by 1, to a minimum of 0. (Gilded Foundry mastery.)
- **Poison** — The defender's Save fails on a natural 6 (a perfect save roll no longer saves).
- **Recover N** — After a failed Parry and Save, roll a D6: a result of N+ saves the retinue. Recover Reroll gives one re-roll. Cannot Recover while Fatigued.
- **Riposte** — A Parry of a natural 6 cancels the Strike and Strikes back once; it is also the only way to Parry a Deadly Strike.
- **Rout** — The army breaks and leaves the Battle (you lose it). A Panic or break check ever modified to 7 or more Routs the whole army automatically.
- **Save** — The defender's roll to avoid a casualty: roll a D6, add the weapon's AP (a negative) and the shield's Save bonus (a positive); the hit is saved on a result ≥ the armor value (Cloth 6+ … Gothic Plate 2+).
- **Seize the Initiative** — Won by the roll-off at the start of the Battle — the winner of their last Battle adds +1 to the roll. You become the Attacker and gain +1 Initiative in the first Skirmish. Some Tactics and the Ministry monument also grant it.
- **Serrated** — Your Strikes worsen the enemy's Recover roll by 1 — enough to deny Recover entirely. (Master Workshop mastery.)
- **Deadly** — A natural 6 to Strike skips the armor Save entirely; only a Riposte can stop it.
- **Shield (−1 to Strike)** — Attackers Striking a shield-bearer take −1 to the Strike roll.
- **Skirmish** — One round of a Battle, run through the numbered Battle steps; a Battle repeats Skirmishes until it ends.
- **Steady** — Your Initiative cannot be reduced by Tactics.
- **Strained** — −1 Initiative every Skirmish (negated by Immune Strain).
- **Strike** — A landed hit. Roll a D6, apply modifiers to the roll, and Strike on a result ≥ the to-Strike number. The target may then Parry, Save, and Recover.
- **Tactic** — A choice both players make secretly and reveal together each Skirmish; it can shift Initiative and Strike rolls.
- **to-Strike number** — The D6 result a retinue needs to Strike (Levy 4+, Man-at-Arms 3+, Sergeant 2+, Knight Templar 3+; lower is better). Bonuses add to the roll; penalties and Fatigue tokens subtract.
- **Unstoppable** — Ignore the target shield's −1 to Strike, and the target's Parry is −1 against you.
- **Unwieldy** — Your Initiative cannot be improved by Tactics.
