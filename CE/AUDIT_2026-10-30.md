- **Repair** glossary L220: "damaged Infrastructure or **Settlements**"; the action (L2890) is Holding or Infrastructure and also offers **Demolish**.

- **Build** notes L2903: "only 1 Monument per settlement". This rule isn't in the rules prose, and the board doesn't flag it. Is it a real rule?

- **Upkeep** glossary L3485: only Build Timers stop upkeep; it should also list Damaged, under repair and Shuttered (already known).
