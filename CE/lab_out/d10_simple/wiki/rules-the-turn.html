The Turn

Each turn runs through five phases in order, then passes the Host and repeats:

  • Empire Phase — Start of the Empire Phase effects, the Season, Timers, Bandit Mechanics, Influence & Envoys, income, Supply and Upkeep, Public Order.
  • End of Empire Phase — each player sends a free Council Envoy of Industry, performing the Era's Council actions; in Spring, a Diplomacy step instead.
  • Envoy Phase — send all Envoys, a Diplomacy step (offers between players), the Intrigue Phase, vote on every Envoy, the Tally Phase, then resolve them all (Prowess → Cunning → Piety → Industry).
  • Battle Phase — Skirmishes, Sieges, and Battles resolve.
  • Rest Phase — cleanup, advance the Season, score Renown, spend one Domain Point, pass the Host.

The Host. Each turn except Spring, one player is the Host. The starting player is always the player directly clockwise from the Host token, including in Spring. The role passes clockwise each Rest Phase (except in Spring). The Host runs most of the Empire Phase's administration: collecting and distributing Trade Income, resolving Bandit Mechanics, and breaking ties (Bandit targeting, and anything not otherwise resolvable). The Host Card carries the step-by-step detail.

Timers

Many actions set a Timer to a number of turns. Each Empire Phase, every active Timer counts down by 1; when a Timer reaches 0 it resolves and is removed (a Timer set to 0 or less resolves immediately). Each action states its own Timer's length — the named types (Build, Repair, Muster, Siege, Sack, Capture, Convert, Truce) are labels for what resolves; the rule is the same for all of them.

Empire Phase

  • Start of the Empire Phase — resolve every effect that happens at the Start of the Empire Phase, including Domain Standing effects (see Domain Standings).
  • Season — apply this turn's Season effect.
  • Timers — count down every active Timer by 1 (Build, Repair, Truce, Siege, Muster, Sack, Capture, in that order), then resolve each Timer that reached 0.
  • Bandit Mechanics (the Host resolves) — grow Bandit Camps by the Era; any camp at 25 Retinues sends out a Bandit Army; Bandit Camps perform Cunning actions; Bandit Armies perform Move actions; spawn new Bandit Camps (in Spring, from Foster Rebellion, or from an Uprising).
  • Influence & Envoys — gain Influence (not in Spring) from the Era, innate modifiers (Trade Partners, Alliances, At War, etc.), Holdings, and Infrastructure; then gain the Era's Envoys.
  • Income & Upkeep — in this order: in Winter only, gain tax income from each of your Settlements by tier (see Settlements); gain Holding income; if there is a Host, the Host collects trade income and distributes it among their Trade Partners; pay Army Supply, Holding and Infrastructure Upkeep, and any other costs; resolve Extort effects; check your Treasury for the Wealth Edict condition.
  • Public Order — total every innate Faith / Doubt source in the Public Order tables and adjust your Public Order by the net change. (Faith and Doubt from actions are applied the moment they're generated; see Public Order.)
  • Endurance — every Army without Strained gains +2 Endurance; then remove Strained from all Armies. (An Army can immediately regain these effects after losing them, if applicable.)
  • Remaining effects — resolve any remaining Each Empire Phase effects.

End of Empire Phase

There is no Council vote: the Council's Domain is Industry. In Spring, the End of Empire Phase is a Diplomacy step instead (as in the Envoy Phase, see Diplomacy step), where each player completes up to 1 agreement in Founding and Ascension and up to 2 from Eminence, each with a different player.

  • Council actions — clockwise from the starting player, each player sends a free Council Envoy of Industry, starting with innate Authority equal to their Industry Standing. Council Envoys auto-Abstain — other players can't Support or Oppose them, though automatic Authority ±X modifiers still apply — and their net Authority can't be lower than 1, so they can't fail or be Condemned. A passed Council Envoy performs actions of that Domain equal to the Era's Council actions per Envoy (see Eras), paying each action's cost as usual (see Envoy Resolution Table for Endorsed refunds).

Envoy Phase

  • Envoy Declaration — all players place every Envoy they have in the Domains of their choice at the same time, then reveal them together.
  • Diplomacy — a brief open discussion of every Envoy on the table: who should be Supported, Opposed, or left alone. Then players make Diplomacy offers to one another, in any order: each offer is a Sign Treaty (name the Treaty) or a Negotiate (state the terms). An accepted offer is a Passed Diplomacy action for both players, and the Treaty is signed at once. Each player completes 1 agreement in this step; once you have, your other offers are withdrawn and you can't accept another. End Treaty needs no answer and also uses it. You may pass instead. The step ends once every player has completed their agreement or passed. Vassals take no part. (Not in Spring.)
  • Intrigue Phase — no talking. The starting player uses all of their Intrigue Phase effects, then each player clockwise does the same, ending with the Host. Each choice is announced and takes effect at once, before the first vote.
  • Vote — no talking. Vote on every Envoy (see Resolving an Envoy): Prowess → Cunning → Piety → Industry, where within each Domain the sender with the highest Domain value has all of their Envoys of that Domain voted on, then the next highest, and so on (ties clockwise from the starting player). A player whose effect says they cast their votes in the Tally Phase skips this step.
  • Tally Phase — once every Envoy has been voted on, in this order:

5.1 Supports — the starting player resolves all of their Tally Phase Supports, then each player clockwise, ending with the Host. Each Support is its own vote, with its own per-vote limit.

5.2 Tally votes — each player who casts their votes in the Tally Phase now votes on every Envoy, in the Vote order.

5.3 Endorsements — the starting player resolves all of their Tally Phase Endorse effects, then each player clockwise, ending with the Host.

5.4 End of the Tally Phase — effects that send another Envoy do so now. Each such Envoy is voted on at once, with players who vote in the Tally Phase voting last, then resolves with the others.

  • Resolve — once the Tally Phase ends, resolve each Envoy in the same order: choose and perform its action according to its net Authority.

All votes are cast before anything resolves, so At War status (for Opposing Industry Envoys) and Treaties are as they stood during the vote.

Battle Phase

If a player performed a Battle or Lay Siege Move action this turn, resolve it now — see Battles & Sieges for the full procedure.

Rest Phase

  • Discard any unused Envoy and Influence tokens.
  • Season — advance the Season by 1.
  • Score — gain 1 Renown, then gain and spend 1 Domain Point.
  • Change Host — rotate the Host token clockwise to the starting player, unless it's Spring.
Glossary · Turn & Phases
TermDefinition
HostHolds the Host Card; the starting player is the player directly clockwise from the Host, including in Spring. See 2.4.2 (The Turn).
EraThe stage of the game, set by Renown: Founding, Ascension, Eminence, Zenith. See 1.3 (Game Structure).
SeasonWinter, Spring, Summer, Fall: one per turn, each with its own effect; the Rest Phase advances it. See 3 (Seasons).
Empire PhaseThe first phase of the turn: Timers, Bandits, Influence and Envoys, income, Supply and Upkeep, then Public Order. See 2.4.4 (Empire Phase).
End of Empire PhaseThe phase after the Empire Phase: each player sends a free Council Envoy of Industry, which Performs the Era's Council actions. See 2.4.5 (End of Empire Phase).
Envoy PhaseEvery player sends their Envoys; they are voted on, then resolved Domain by Domain. See 2.4.6 (Envoy Phase).
Intrigue PhaseThe Envoy Phase step after the Diplomacy step, before the first Vote. See 2.4.6.3 (Envoy Phase).
Tally PhaseThe Envoy Phase step after every Envoy has been voted on: Supports, then the Votes of players who vote in the Tally Phase, then Endorsements, then effects that send another Envoy (voted at once). See 2.4.6.5 (Envoy Phase).
Battle PhaseBattles and Sieges begun this turn resolve.
Rest PhaseThe end of the turn: discard unused Envoys and Influence, advance the Season, score Renown and a Domain Point, pass the Host. See 2.4.8 (Rest Phase).
