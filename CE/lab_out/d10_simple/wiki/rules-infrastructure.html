Infrastructure

Infrastructure is the foundation of your Settlements. Each active Infrastructure provides an effect that applies to your entire empire and all its Settlements.

Example: Wooden Walls defend every one of your Settlements, and a Cathedral gives you Faith +1 each turn, however many Settlements you have.

Infrastructure comes in 5 ascending tiers. Primitive Infrastructure builds the basic blocks of your empire. Developed Infrastructure expands and replaces some Primitive Infrastructure. Sophisticated Infrastructure allows more powerful effects and helps unlock unique Monument Holdings. Prodigious Infrastructure comes next, and Wondrous Infrastructure — the world Wonders — is the highest tier.

To build any Infrastructure, you need at least one active Infrastructure of the tier below it — so Developed needs one Primitive, Sophisticated needs one Developed (and therefore one Primitive), and so on up to Wondrous. One Tier Primitive means any one Primitive Infrastructure. Some Infrastructure also names its own requirement on top of this: a Library needs a Town Hall, and a Magistrate's Hall needs a Library.

Wonders are the Wondrous tier, with Realm Scope: each is built once per game and needs the Infrastructure named in its requirement. They're Edict-satisfying buildings with very powerful effects. A Wonder exists inside your capital and can't be Razed or Damaged. Your capital can hold every Wonder, but you build one Wonder at a time: you can't start a Wonder while another of yours is under its Build Timer.
