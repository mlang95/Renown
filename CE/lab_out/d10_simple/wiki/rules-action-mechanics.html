Action Mechanics

Sending an Envoy

Envoys are the currency of actions: to perform an action you send an Envoy during the Envoy Phase. Sending costs 1 Envoy, and you must be able to pay the action's cost before you send it. Declare the Domain but not the action — "I'd like to send a [Domain] Envoy." Envoys never target: you choose the action, and its target, only after the Envoy passes, so no one knows exactly what will happen until then.

Resolving an Envoy

Your Envoy begins with Authority equal to your innate Authority in its Domain — Untested 1, Rising 2, Established 3, Sovereign 4 — plus any automatic Authority ±X modifiers. (These come from your Holdings, Infrastructure, and Heritage, and apply on their own; you never spend Influence for them.) Diplomacy has no Standing; a Diplomacy Envoy begins with Authority set by the Era (Founding 1, Ascension 2, Eminence 3, Zenith 4).

Then, clockwise from the starting player and skipping the sender, every other player may respond, with no talking during the vote:

  • Support X — spend X of your Influence to raise the Envoy's net Authority.
  • Oppose X — spend X of your Influence to lower it.
  • Abstain — spend nothing.

You spend Influence only when you Support or Oppose; abstaining is free. You can't vote on your own Envoy. On a single vote, each player may spend up to their Standing in that Domain (Untested 1, Rising 2, Established 3, Sovereign 4). On Diplomacy Envoys the cap is set by the Era instead (Founding 1, Ascension 2, Eminence 3, Zenith 4). Holdings, Infrastructure, and Heritages may raise these caps. Unused Influence is discarded in the Rest Phase.

Each player declares in turn, so the running net Authority is public — later voters can react to it. The Host, sitting just before the starting player, votes last on every Envoy they didn't send.

Why this is the heart of the game: at Untested your Envoy starts at 1, and you can't support it yourself — so it can fail if another player opposes you, and be saved if another supports you. Unless players back each other, Envoys fall to cheap opposition; deciding whose actions to lift and whose to bury is the core of every turn.

Envoy Resolution Table

Add everything together for the net Authority, then read the outcome:

Net AuthorityResultEffect
−3 or lessCondemnedResolve that Domain's Condemn effect
0 or lessFailedGain Doubt 1
1+PassedPerform the action's Pass effect
3+EndorsedAlso perform the Endorsed effect

Effects that modify a specific action's Authority — including effects on actions targeting a player — apply after the net Authority is totalled, once the action and its target are chosen. They can raise or lower the result: a would-be pass can become a fail, or be Condemned. On a would-be fail, the player may instead perform a different action of the same Domain targeting the same player or Settlement.

When you perform an action, pay its cost (gold or Doubt) and follow the pass effect. If the action is endorsed, also perform the Domain's endorsed effect.

TermDefinition
InfluenceThe political currency of voting. See 5.2 (Influence).

Personal EnvoyAn Envoy you send in the Envoy Phase to attempt to perform an action; the amount of Envoys you can spend and the volume of actions you can perform with one scales with Era.
Council PhaseBefore Personal Envoys, a brief Forum, then all players vote on a Domain with no talking (clockwise; Host breaks ties). See 2.4.5 (Council Phase).
Council EnvoyA free Envoy resolved in the Council Phase on the voted Domain; auto-Abstained, net Authority floored at 1. See 2.4.5.3 (Council Phase).
Intrigue PhaseThe Envoy Phase step after the Forum, before the first vote. See 2.4.6.3 (Envoy Phase).
VoteOn each Envoy, every player except the sender, in clockwise order from the starting player, must Support, Oppose, or Abstain. See 5.1.2.2 (Resolving an Envoy).
Support XSpend X Influence to increase an Envoy's net Authority. See 5.1.2.2 (Resolving an Envoy).
Oppose XSpend X Influence to decrease an Envoy's net Authority. See 5.1.2.2 (Resolving an Envoy).
AbstainDecline to spend Influence on a vote. See 5.1.2.2 (Resolving an Envoy).
Tally PhaseThe Envoy Phase step after every Envoy has been voted on: Supports, then the votes of players who vote in the Tally Phase, then Endorsements, then effects that send another Envoy (voted at once). See 2.4.6.5 (Envoy Phase).
Net AuthorityThe sum of an Envoy's starting Authority (1+ by Standing) and all Support, Oppose, and Authority X. See 5.1.3 (Envoy Resolution Table).
Envoy OutcomeHow a Sent Envoy resolves, by Net Authority: Condemned (<= −3), Failed (−3 < Net <= 0, gain Doubt 1), Passed (>= 1), Endorsed (>= 3). See 5.1.3 (Envoy Resolution Table).
