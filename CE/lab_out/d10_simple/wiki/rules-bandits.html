Bandits

Outlaw Country

Outlaw Country is zones of Territory no player can Control, where Bandit Camps spawn. Setup places zones in two ways; after Setup, every zone works the same.

  • Your zone (Deploy): demarcate 3 Territories in your starting region, each within Range 1 of at least one other Outlaw Country Territory and at least Range 2 from any Settlement — the first exactly Range 2 from your Town, and none within Range 2 of your Hamlet. If that's not possible, place them Range 2 from your capital Settlement.
  • Wild zones (last Setup step): the Host scatters more zones over the unclaimed board — the number of players ÷ 0, rounded down, + 0. Each is 3 connected Territories, none on mountain or water Territory, at least Range 3 from every player's Province and at least Range 5 from every other Outlaw Country zone. Each Province starts the game with exactly one Outlaw Country zone.

A zone is your Outlaw Country while any of its Territories is within your Province. A zone in no player's Province belongs to no one: its camps spawn and grow, but take no action until they're within a player's Province; a camp that grows into a Bandit Army Marches like any other.

Spawning Bandit Camps

In the Empire Phase each Spring: place a Bandit Camp of 10 Bandits (Retinues) with 2000 gold in each Outlaw Country zone.

Note: if an Outlaw Country is chosen to receive another Bandit Camp but there's no room to place one, instead each existing Bandit Camp within that outlaw country grows according to the current Era. An Uprising places as many of its camps as fit; the camps there then grow once for each camp that couldn't be placed. Foster Rebellion is the exception: it expands that Outlaw Country by one Territory and places the camp there.

Growing Bandit Camps

A Bandit Camp grows each Empire Phase by the Era's Retinues per turn. When it reaches 25 Retinues it sends out a Bandit Army of 25, and the camp stays on its Territory. While its Army is out, the camp doesn't grow or send out another Army, and no camp can spawn on its Territory. The camp can't be attacked while its Army is out. After each Battle it doesn't lose, the Army returns to its camp, and the camp re-musters toward 25 from the Retinues that come back; a Bandit Army that loses a Battle is destroyed, and its camp with it. Its Retinues carry the Era's armament.

Ascension2Levy: Pitchfork + Padded
Eminence3Levy: Spear + Buckler Shield + Leather

Bandit Rules

Bandits share the same Renown level as players. A Bandit's Domain value is set by the size of its Bandit Camp:

  • For every 5 Retinues in the Bandit Camp or Army, the Bandit Camp has +2 Cunning and +2 Prowess.

All players are At War with all Bandits and their camps. Players abstain on all Bandit actions, but innate modifiers can still apply and cause the action to Fail.

While there are 10+ Bandits (Retinues) in a camp, roll a d10 each turn:

d10Cunning Action
1–4Raze
5–6Intercept Caravan
7–8Sabotage
9–10Destabilize

A camp's action targets the Settlements and Armies of every player whose Province covers the camp, the nearest first. If the camp is in no player's Province, it takes no action.

A Bandit Camp keeps Extorted money in its Treasury and doesn't pay costs, upkeep, or recoup. When you destroy a Bandit Camp or Army, you Extort its whole Treasury (a Bandit Army's includes its camp's).

Bandit Army Behavior

After Bandit Mechanics resolve, a Bandit Army performs a Move action based on, in order:

  • Skirmish — if another player's Army is in range.
  • Pillage — if another player's Settlement is in range: end adjacent to it. Each Empire Phase, in the Extort step, the Bandit Army Extorts half of that Settlement's Holding income into its camp's Treasury, until it fights a Battle or leaves. A Settlement can have only one Bandit Army Pillaging it.
  • March — toward the closest player's Army or Settlement.

Note: if range ties, the Host chooses the target.

Attacking a Bandit Camp

Use the Move action to end adjacent to a Bandit Camp, then resolve it as a Battle in the Battle Phase. Another player rolls the Bandits' Tactic each Skirmish and makes their to-Strike and Save rolls:

d10Tactic
1Scout
2Ambush
3Flank
4–6Charge
7–8Fighting Formation
9–10Defensive Formation

Winning the Battle destroys the Bandit Camp, whether its Bandits are wiped out or Rout: Extort its whole Treasury (your Spoils of War come out of it). Bandits never Fall Back, but they may Rout as normal.
