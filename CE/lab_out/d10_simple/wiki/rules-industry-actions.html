Industry Actions

ActionCostRequiresEffect (if passes)Endorsed
Improve2000 gold—Choose an unlocked, available Infrastructure and set a Build Timer equal to its build time. When it completes, the Infrastructure becomes active in every Settlement in your Province. — You must have at least one Infrastructure from the prior tier before building the next tier.; A Wonder is built in your capital.Recoup 2000.
Repair0 gold—Choose a Damaged Holding or Infrastructure in a Settlement you control and set Repair Timer 2. When it reaches 0, choose one: Restore — reactivate it with all effects; Demolish — remove the Holding tile from your Empire Tableau.Recoup 0.
Build2000 gold—Choose an inactive Settlement Ward you control, then choose a Holding with one complete line of its Mastery Chain under your control (and meet its other requirements) and set a Build Timer equal to its build time (usually 1). When it completes, place the Holding in an available Ward slot; its effect activates immediately. — You may have only 1 Monument Holdings per settlement.; Holdings have Build Timers according to their required Standing. Ungated: 1, Rising: 2, Established: 3, & Sovereign: 4.; Build Timer by Standing: Untested 1, Rising 2, Established 3, Sovereign 4.Recoup 2000.
Settle2000 goldRising IndustryCharter a new Village on a Hill in your Province or within the Presence of one of your Armies, range 2+ from Outlaw Country, and set a Build Timer 2.Recoup 2000.
Expand2000 goldRising IndustryIncrease a Settlement by one tier (Village to Town, Town to City, City to Metropolis), raising its Wards, Tax income, Muster Limit and Reach. — To upgrade to City or Metropolis you must meet that era requirement; otherwise set Build Timer 1.Recoup 2000.

Rule: Ulterior Motive

Unless you are At War with the player sending it, you can't Oppose an Industry Envoy.
