Diplomacy & Treaties

Treaties & Alliances

TreatySigned ViaEraEffect
Peace TreatySign TreatyAnyEnds war between the two players; sets Truce Timer 5.
Trade AgreementSign TreatyAnyBegin trading (Trade Income flows from next turn). Requires active Dirt Roads or a Can Trade effect for each player.
Non-Aggression PactSign TreatyAnyNeither player may Declare War on the other. Ending it via End Treaty gives both a Truce Timer 5.
Military AllianceSign TreatyEminenceMutual offensive pact: if a member Declares War, all members Declare War on the target next turn (and send 1 fewer envoy).
Defensive AllianceSign TreatyZenithMutual defense: attacking one member puts all members at war with the attacker.

Alliance Rules:

  • New alliance members are added by unanimous agreement.
  • A player cannot be in more than one alliance at a time.
  • An alliance may remove a member via End Treaty if all others agree.

Vassalization

To vassalize a player:

  • Control that player's capital Settlement while the player has no other Settlements or Armies under their Control.
  • At the end of the Rest Phase, return every Settlement you Control that was chartered by that player to that player. You become their Suzerain, and they become your Vassal.

While Suzerain:

  • The Vassal immediately ends all existing Treaties and inherits every Treaty the Suzerain holds. For the rest of the game the Vassal's Treaties mirror the Suzerain's exactly — any Treaty the Suzerain signs, ends, or has ended for them applies identically to the Vassal. The Vassal counts as being in a military and defensive Alliance with the Suzerain, but has no say in other players joining the Suzerain's Alliances.
  • The Vassal can't perform Diplomacy actions and can't be the target of Diplomacy actions.
  • The Suzerain collects:
    • Half of the Vassal's trade income from trading with other players.
    • The first 3 Influence the Vassal generates each turn.

Mutual exchange. Each Empire Phase, if both players agree, either player may exchange up to 5000 gold, exchange ownership of Settlements, or transfer forces (so long as the Armies are adjacent to each other, or an Army is adjacent to a Garrison), in either direction.

If the Suzerain is vassalized, both the original Vassal and the Suzerain become Vassals of the new Suzerain, and the new Suzerain gains the benefits of each Vassal.

Glossary · Treaties
TermDefinition
TreatyA standing agreement between players (e.g. Trade Agreement, Non-Aggression, Alliance) signed via Diplomacy. See 7.1 (Treaties & Alliances).
AllianceA Treaty binding players to mutual support; Military and Defensive Alliances unlock by Era. See 7.1 (Treaties & Alliances).
At WarCannot trade; can be the target of Siege, Battle, & Pillage actions from players At War with this player.
VassalA player subordinated via Vassalization: their chartered settlements return to them, they mirror the Suzerain's Treaties, count as allied to the Suzerain, and cannot perform or be targeted by Diplomacy actions. See 7.2 (Vassalization).
SuzerainThe player who has Vassalized another; the vassal mirrors their Treaties. See 7.2 (Vassalization).
