Settlements

Settlements are the primary vehicle of your Empire. They come in tiers: Village, Town, City, and a capital Metropolis. Each tier provides tax income each Winter, Settlement Wards that a Holding can fill, and a per-turn Muster limit of Retinues. The values per tier are below:

Settlement Wards

A Settlement Ward is a slot inside a Settlement that holds one Holding; Wards are never placed on the map. A Settlement has one Ward per tier: a Village has 1, a Town 2, a City 3 and a Metropolis 4. A Hamlet has 3 Wards.

Chartering Settlements

When you Settle a new Village, place it in your Province, at least Range 4 from all other Settlements and Range 2 from any Outlaw Country. You may only Settle on grassland Territory.

Settlement Reach

Settlements have a Reach value set by their tier, from 2 up to 8. Reach X determines which Territory you Control in your Province: at Reach 1 you Control all Territory within Range 1 of that Settlement. If more than one player Controls a Territory, it's Contested — Controlled by both.

Hamlets

Hamlets are unique Settlements, placed exactly Range 2 from your capital at the start of the game. These small farmland communities produce no tax income or Muster limit, but have 3 Settlement Wards. You may only build Natural Holdings in a Hamlet, and a Holding in a Hamlet can only share a Ward with its parent if it is itself Natural. You may also build Arable Land in a Hamlet even if that Raw Material isn't in the Hamlet's region.

TermDefinition
Settlement WardA slot in a Settlement that holds one Holding; a Settlement has one Ward per tier (a Hamlet has 3 Wards, Natural Holdings only). See 8.1.2 (Settlement Wards).
HoldingAn object that is placed into a Settlement Ward or onto a Root Holding as a result of a Build action, giving effects which affect your Empire in many ways.
RootA Holding with no Mastery Chain requirement: you may start it without controlling a parent. See 2.2.13b (Core Principles).
NaturalNatural Holdings do not cost Upkeep & can be used in Hamlets.
Mastery ChainA Holding's Mastery Chain is the line of Holdings it builds from, usually running from a Raw Material up to a Monument. See 2.2.13 (Core Principles).
Mastery Chain XWhile this Holding occupies the same Settlement Ward as X (the Raw Material or Holding named on its tile), it uses no ward of its own — the two share one ward. See 2.2.13 (Core Principles).
