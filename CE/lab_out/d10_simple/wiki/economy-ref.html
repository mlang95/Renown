Economy Reference

Upkeep — three separate tracks

Each upkeep pool is reduced by different effects; a reducer that names one track does not touch the others.

TrackHow it works
HoldingEach Empire Phase: by Standing: Untested 100 (Build Timer 1), Rising 200 (Build Timer 2), Established 300 (Build Timer 3), Sovereign 400 (Build Timer 4); Natural 0.
ArmyΣ(each Army's cost by Retinue type) - Upkeep −X. Reduced by Levy Hall, Tannery, Saddlery, Butchery, Smokehouse, Advanced Blast Furnace, High Quartermaster, etc.
InfrastructurePer-Empire upkeep in INFRASTRUCTURE. Masonry removes Primitive; Trade Guild removes Developed; College of Engineering removes Sophisticated.

Holding upkeep by type: Monument 300, Power 200, Energy/Natural 0, all others 100.
