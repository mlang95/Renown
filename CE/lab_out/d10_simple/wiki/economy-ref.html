Economy Reference

Supply and Upkeep — three separate tracks

Each cost is reduced by different effects; a reducer that names one track does not touch the others.

TrackHow it works
Holdings (Upkeep)Each Empire Phase: by Standing: Untested 100 (Build Timer 0), Rising 200 (Build Timer 1), Established 300 (Build Timer 2), Sovereign 400 (Build Timer 3); Natural 0.
Armies (Supply)Σ(each Army's cost by Retinue type) - Supply −X. Reduced by Levy Hall, Tannery, Saddlery, Butchery, Smokehouse, Advanced Blast Furnace, High Quartermaster, etc.
Infrastructure (Upkeep)Listed per Infrastructure; Upkeep −X lowers Holding and Infrastructure Upkeep. Masonry removes Primitive; Trade Guild removes Developed; College of Engineering removes Sophisticated.

Holding Upkeep by type: Monument 300, Power 200, Energy/Natural 0, all others 100.
