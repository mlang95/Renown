Battle Phase

A Battle is fought as a series of Skirmishes, run through the steps below until one side is wiped out, Routs, or successfully Falls Back. Full keyword detail is in the glossary tables at the end of this section.

Beginning a Battle

  • Declare Army — declare which Army will Skirmish (if more than one is adjacent to either Army in the Battle).
  • Seize the Initiative — the player who performed the Battle action usually gains Seize the Initiative, but Terrain and the Ministry of Military Strategy can change that. That player gains Initiative +1 in the first Skirmish.

The Skirmish Steps

  • Form the line. Each side places up to 10 Retinues in its front line — one Strike die each — and may keep up to 5 in reserve to replace losses as they fall. An Army of 25 keeps the remainder in camp; between Skirmishes, camp and reserves refill the front line and reserve to their maximums.
  • Choose Tactics and equipment. Both players secretly pick one Tactic and, if they have a choice, the equipment they'll use this Skirmish (an Army carrying both a Ranged and a Melee weapon picks one; a Bastard Sword picks its 1H or 2H profile; an Army with Guige Strap picks its 1H Weapon and Shield or its 2H Weapon; an Army with Florentine may Dual Wield its 1H Melee Weapon instead of using its Shield), then reveal together. No one may Fall Back in the first Skirmish. A One-Shot weapon may only be Equipped in the first Skirmish; if it isn't, it can't be used that Battle. Only Equipped gear counts: while a 2H weapon is Equipped your Shield gives nothing that Skirmish.
  • Initiative. Add your Initiative bonuses from Tactics, weapon profiles and any other source, to a maximum of +2 (only the Ministry of Military Strategy raises it), then subtract your penalties. Initiative has no minimum. The higher Initiative Strikes first this Skirmish. If tied, both armies strike simultaneously. Resolve this taking turns rolling strikes and saves, but do not apply casualties until both sides have fought. At −2 or lower you Blunder — your to-Strike is set to 10+.
  • Strike. The side with the higher Initiative rolls a D10 for each front-line Retinue, applying its modifiers. It Strikes on a Modified roll ≥ its to-Strike number, which is never worse than 10+ (Levy 6+, Man-at-Arms 5+, Sergeant 3+, Oathsworn Knight 4+). An Unmodified 10 may trigger Cleave, Deadly, or Destroy Shield.
  • Defend. For each Strike, a defender with Parry may Parry (D10, 8+ cancels; an Unmodified 10 Ripostes if the defender has Riposte), then Saves (a Modified roll: D10 + the weapon's AP + the shield's bonus ≥ the armor value), then a defender with Heal may Heal (after a failed Save, a final D10 ≥ the Heal value). Unsaved, unrecovered Strikes are casualties, and leave the field at once. Then the other side Strikes, and is defended against, the same way, if able.
  • Panic check. After both sides have Struck, each side that took 5 or more casualties this Skirmish (Strikes it Healed still count, unless it has Enduring) takes a Panic check: roll its Morale, 5 dice; failures are casualties. A check ever modified to 11 or more Routs the whole Army.
  • Fatigue. Each side that fought loses 1 Endurance; a side at 0 Endurance is Fatigued. Each Fatigued side's field then takes a Break check — roll 5 D10, each ≥ its modified Morale value; failures are casualties, and a Break check never triggers a Panic check — and then gains a Fatigue token: each token is −2 to that Army's Morale rolls; tokens stack and last until the Battle ends. A check ever modified to 11 or more Routs the whole Army.
  • End the Skirmish. Both sides first finish applying casualties, their Panic checks and their Break checks; only then is the winner decided. The Battle ends if a side is wiped out, Routs (modified Morale 11+), or successfully Falls Back. Otherwise refill the lines and begin the next Skirmish.

Ending a Battle

  • Clean up — remove all Fatigue tokens.
  • Spoils of War — the player who won Extorts the unmodified cost of the opposing Army, by its Retinue type.

Glossary · Battle Structure
TermDefinition
BattleA collection of Skirmishes between two players, continuing until a side's Retinue count is reduced to 0, Routs, or successfully Falls Back, ending the Battle.
SkirmishOne round of a Battle, run through the numbered Battle steps; a Battle repeats Skirmishes until it ends. See 11.1.3 (The Skirmish Steps).
Attacker / DefenderThe Attacker performed the Move action that caused the Battle or Lay Siege; the other side is the Defender.
Seize the InitiativeInitiative +1 in the first Skirmish. See 11.1.2.2 (Beginning a Battle).
TacticA choice both players make secretly and reveal together each Skirmish; it can shift Initiative, Strike, and Save rolls. See 11.1.5 (Tactic Matrix).
FieldYour Retinues in play — front line (up to 10) plus reserve (up to 5). See 11.1.3.1 (The Skirmish Steps).
CasualtyA Retinue removed from the Field — from an unsaved Strike or a failed Panic or Break check.
InitiativeDecides who Strikes first each Skirmish (higher first). See 11.1.3.3 (The Skirmish Steps).
BlunderAt Initiative −2 or lower, your to-Strike is set to 10+ (penalties can't make it worse). See 11.1.3.3 (The Skirmish Steps).
StrikeA landed hit. See 11.1.3.4 (The Skirmish Steps).
to-Strike numberThe D10 result a Retinue needs to Strike (see the Retinue table; lower is better). See 11.1.3.4 (The Skirmish Steps).
APArmor Penetration — a weapon's (negative) modifier to the defender's Save roll; the more negative, the harder to save.
SaveThe defender's roll to avoid a Casualty: roll a D10, add the weapon's AP (a negative) and the shield's Save bonus (a positive); the hit is saved on a Modified roll >= the armor value. See 11.1.3.5 (The Skirmish Steps).
EnduranceA side's stamina. See 11.1.3.7 (The Skirmish Steps).
FatiguedA side at 0 Endurance. See 11.1.3.7 (The Skirmish Steps).
Fatigue TokenEach token reduces Morale by 2; if your modified Morale is ever 11+, your Army Routs. See 11.1.3.7 (The Skirmish Steps).
MoraleHow steady a Retinue is when tested (lower is steadier; see the Retinue table). Break and Panic checks roll it: a D10 per Retinue in the Field, up to 5 dice, each must meet its modified value; failures are Casualties. If the modified value is ever 11+, the Army Routs.
Break checkTaken by each Fatigued side's Field every Skirmish, just before it gains its Fatigue Token. See 11.1.3.7 (The Skirmish Steps).
Panic checkTaken at most once per Skirmish by a side that suffered 5 or more Casualties in that Skirmish (Healed Strikes count, unless Enduring), after both sides have Struck. See 11.1.3.6 (The Skirmish Steps).
RoutThe Army breaks and leaves the Battle. See 11.1.3.8 (The Skirmish Steps).
Fall BackA controlled retreat that ends the Battle with at least one Retinue left — a partial success.
Glossary · Combat Keywords — Defense
TermDefinition
ParryIf your Army has gained Parry, roll a D10 to attempt to Parry a Strike before an Armor Save. On an 8+ (better with Parry +1), the Strike is Parried and has no further effect. A Parry that would need more than 10+ can't be made.
Parry +1Your Parry rolls succeed on one lower. Each +1 stacks.
RiposteIf your Army has gained Riposte and rolls a Focused Parry against a Melee Weapon's Strike, you Riposte: your opponent immediately takes a Strike from your melee weapon. You can Riposte a Riposte.
Riposte +1You also Riposte on a successful Parry roll one lower than Focused (9).
HealIf your Army has gained Heal and a to-Save roll fails, roll a D10 & compare it to your Heal value: a result greater than or equal to your Heal value recovers the Retinue. Healed Strikes still count toward the Panic check threshold.
EnduringSuccessfully Healed Strikes don't count toward the Panic check threshold.
ShieldedA cumulative −1 penalty to the Strike roll (to a maximum of 10+). Sources: a shield's −1 to Strike. Shielded 2 (Heater Shield) is −2; Shielded 3 (Tower Shield) is −3. Hook X ignores up to X of it.
GussetedA Focused Save succeeds, regardless of AP.
ArticulatedStrikes against this Army have their AP reduced by 1. The Army is also Immune Strained.
Shield WallWhile this Army plays Defensive Formation with a Shield Equipped, Strikes against it have their AP reduced by 1. Granted by Pavisiers' Yard.
Glossary · Combat Keywords — Offense
TermDefinition
RangedEvery Ranged Weapon counts as Deadly, Unstoppable, Awkward and Negate Riposte. Immune to one of them removes only that part.
DeadlyOn a Focused Strike: that Strike has AP −10, the defender can at best Parry it on a Focused roll, and it can't be Healed.
Deadly +1Your Deadly also triggers on an Unmodified roll one lower than Focused (9+). Each +1 stacks. Written "(Ranged Weapons)", it applies only while a Ranged Weapon is Equipped.
CleaveOn a Focused Strike: roll one extra Strike die at your modified to-Strike; a Focused extra die Strikes and rolls another.
Cleave +1Your Cleave also triggers on an Unmodified roll one lower than Focused (9+). Each +1 stacks.
PoisonWhen the Defender receives a Strike and rolls a Focused Save, it fails; the resulting wound may only be Healed with a Focused Heal.
Destroy ShieldOn a Focused Strike: the target loses its Shield attributes for the rest of the Battle.
Unstoppable−2 to the defender's Parry roll.
SerratedA cumulative −2 penalty to the defender's Heal roll.
Hook XYour Strikes ignore up to X modifiers of the defender's Shielded.
WedgeIn the first Skirmish of a Battle, −1 to the defender's Parry roll against Strikes from the weapon that gained it.
First Charge XIn the first Skirmish of a Battle, Cavalry Spear and Lances gain −X AP. First Charge with no number is First Charge 1; each source adds up.
Glossary · Combat Keywords — Handling
TermDefinition
EquippedThe weapon chosen for the Skirmish.
SteadyInitiative cannot be reduced by Tactics.
UnwieldyInitiative cannot be improved by Tactics.
BracedInitiative cannot be modified by Tactics. Counts as Steady and Unwieldy: Immune Unwieldy leaves Steady.
NimbleGain Initiative +1 in the first Skirmish of each Battle.
DrilledDoes not lose Endurance in the first Skirmish of each Battle.
2HCannot use a Shield: while a 2H weapon is Equipped, your Shield gives nothing that Skirmish (no Save bonus, Initiative or keywords).
Dual-equipCarry two weapons at once (e.g. melee + ranged). Granted by Training Grounds.
FlorentineYour Armies may equip two of the same 1H Melee Weapon. If they do, a failed Parry is rerolled once, but rerolled parries cannot Riposte. Gain Two-Handed.
AwkwardWhile Equipped with this weapon during a Skirmish, you cannot Parry.
CrudeWhile this weapon or shield is Equipped during a Skirmish, you cannot Parry, and so cannot Riposte. Immune Awkward doesn't remove this.
One-ShotMay only be Equipped in the first Skirmish of a Battle; if it isn't Equipped then, it can't be used that Battle. Requires Training Grounds.
Shield InitiativeShield Initiative penalty reduced by 1: your Shield's negative Initiative is 1 smaller (a Shield with no penalty gains nothing). Granted by Shieldwright.
Buckler with 2HArmies Equipped with a 2H Weapon may Equip a Buckler Shield, and it counts while the 2H Weapon is Equipped. Granted by Joinery.
Guige StrapYour Armies may carry a 2H Melee Weapon as well as a 1H Melee Weapon and Shield, and choose each Skirmish which to Equip: the 1H Weapon and Shield, or the 2H Weapon. An Army carrying both may not carry a Ranged Weapon. Granted by Buckler School.
Glossary · Combat Keywords — Immune / Negate
TermDefinition
Immune [keyword]Can't gain that keyword's effect as it applies to your Army (e.g. Immune Strained, Destroy Shield, Steady, etc.).
Negate [keyword]Your opponent can't gain that keyword's effect as it applies to their Army (e.g. Negate Shielded).
