Battle Phase

Begin Battle

Begin Skirmish

  • Declare Army — declare which Army will Skirmish (if more than one is adjacent to either Army in the Battle).
  • Choose a Tactic and equipment — place a face-down Tactic, together with your equipment for this Skirmish if you have a choice (a Tiltyard, or a Bastard Sword's 1H/2H profile); reveal both once the other player has too. A One-Shot weapon must be Equipped in the first Skirmish.
  • Resolve Tactic modifiers — after Tactics are revealed, apply the Initiative and any other modifiers from your Tactics and weapon profile.

How a Battle Resolves

A Battle is fought as a series of Skirmishes, run through the steps below until one side is wiped out, Routs, or successfully Falls Back. Full keyword detail is in the glossary tables at the end of this section.

Begin the Battle — Seize the Initiative

Determine who gains Seize the Initiative. Typically, the player who performed the Battle Action gains Seize the Initiative, but Terrain and the Ministry of Military Strategy can affect that. When a player gains Seize the Initiative, the player gains +1 Initiative in the first round of combat.

The Skirmish Steps

  • Form the line. Each side places up to 10 Retinues in its front line — one Strike die each — and may keep up to 5 in reserve to replace losses as they fall. An Army of 25 keeps the remainder in camp; between Skirmishes, camp and reserves refill the front line and reserve to their maximums.
  • Choose Tactics and equipment. Both players secretly pick one Tactic and, if they have a choice, the equipment they'll use this Skirmish (a Tiltyard Army picks its Ranged or Melee weapon; a Bastard Sword picks its 1H or 2H profile), then reveal together. A One-Shot weapon must be Equipped in the first Skirmish and can't be Equipped after. Only Equipped gear counts: while a 2H weapon is Equipped your Shield gives nothing that Skirmish.
  • Initiative. Initiative ranges from -2 to +2. The higher Initiative Strikes first this Skirmish. At -2 or lower you Blunder — your to-Strike is set to 10+, before other negative modifiers.
  • Roll to Strike. Roll a D10 for each front-line Retinue, applying its modifiers. It Strikes on a result ≥ its to-Strike number (Levy 6+, Man-at-Arms 5+, Sergeant 3+, Oathsworn 4+). A natural 10 may trigger Cleave, Deadly, or Destroy Shield.
  • Strike and defend. Resolve the first side's Strikes — the defender may Parry (D10, 8+ cancels; a natural 10 is a Riposte), then Save (D10 + the weapon's AP + the shield's bonus ≥ the armor value), then Heal (after a failed Save, a final D10 ≥ the Heal value). Unsaved, unrecovered Strikes are casualties, and leave the field at once.
  • Strike back. The other side Strikes the same way, if able.
  • Panic check. After both sides have Struck, each side that took more than 5 casualties this Skirmish (Strikes it Healed still count, unless it has Enduring) takes a Panic check: roll its Morale, up to 5 dice; failures are casualties. A check ever modified to 11 or more Routs the whole Army.
  • Lose Endurance. Each side that fought loses 1 Endurance. A side at 0 Endurance is Fatigued.
  • Break check. Before gaining its token, each Fatigued side's field takes a Break check: roll a D10 per Retinue in the field, up to 5 dice, each ≥ its modified Morale value; failures are casualties. A Break check never triggers a Panic check. A check ever modified to 11 or more Routs the whole Army.
  • Fatigue token. Each Fatigued side then gains a Fatigue token — each token is −2 to that Army's Morale rolls; Tokens stack and last until the Battle ends.
  • End the Skirmish. The Battle ends if a side is wiped out, Routs (modified Morale 11+), or successfully Falls Back. Otherwise refill the lines and begin the next Skirmish.

Resolve Battle

  • Remove all Fatigue tokens.
  • The player who won extorts spoils of war.

Spoils of War: extort the total cost of the Retinues, by type, you destroyed in the Battle.

End Battle

Glossary · Battle Structure
TermDefinition
BattleOne fight between two players, resolved as a series of Skirmishes until a side is wiped out, Routs, or Falls Back.
SkirmishOne round of a Battle, run through the numbered Battle steps; a Battle repeats Skirmishes until it ends.
Attacker / DefenderThe Attacker performed the Move action that caused the Battle or Lay Siege; the other side is the Defender. Each Skirmish the Attacker declares equipment first; the Defender then responds.
Seize the InitiativeTypically, the player who performed the Battle Action gains Seize the Initiative, but Terrain and the Ministry of Military Strategy can affect that. When a player gains Seize the Initiative, the player gains +1 Initiative in the first round of combat.
TacticA choice both players make secretly and reveal together each Skirmish; it can shift Initiative, Strike, and Save rolls.
FieldYour retinues in play — front line (up to 10) plus reserve (up to 5). Casualties leave the field at once, lowering its count.
CasualtyA retinue removed from the field — from an unsaved Strike or a failed Panic or Break check.
InitiativeDecides who Strikes first each Skirmish (higher first). Runs -2 to +2 (Ministry can raise the maximum to +3). At -2 or lower you Blunder.
BlunderAt Initiative -2, your to-Strike is set to 10+, before other negative modifiers.
StrikeA landed hit. Roll a D10, apply modifiers to the roll, and Strike on a result >= the to-Strike number. The target may then Parry, Save, and Heal.
to-Strike numberThe D10 result a retinue needs to Strike (see the retinue table; lower is better). Bonuses add to the roll; penalties and Fatigue tokens subtract.
APArmor Penetration — a weapon's (negative) modifier to the defender's Save roll; the more negative, the harder to save.
SaveThe defender's roll to avoid a casualty: roll a D10, add the weapon's AP (a negative) and the shield's Save bonus (a positive); the hit is saved on a result >= the armor value.
EnduranceA side's stamina. Each side that fights loses 1 per Skirmish; at 0 it becomes Fatigued.
FatiguedA side at 0 Endurance. Each Skirmish its field takes a Break check, then it gains a Fatigue token.
Fatigue TokenEach token reduces Morale by 2 (uncapped); if your modified Morale is ever 11+, your army Routs. Tokens stack.
MoraleHow steady a retinue is when tested (lower is steadier; see the retinue table). Break and Panic checks roll it: a D10 per retinue in the field, up to 5 dice, each must meet its modified value; failures are casualties. If the modified value is ever 11+, the army Routs.
Break checkTaken by each Fatigued side's field every Skirmish, just before it gains its Fatigue token. Roll Morale (5 dice, modified by Fatigue tokens); failures are casualties, but a Break check never triggers a Panic check. Unbreakable auto-passes.
Panic checkTaken at most once per Skirmish by a side that suffered more than 5 casualties in that Skirmish (Healed Strikes count, unless Enduring), after both sides have Struck. Roll Morale (5 dice); Immune Panic auto-passes.
RoutThe army breaks and leaves the Battle (you lose it). Whenever an army's modified Morale value reaches 11+ or more, it Routs automatically.
Fall BackA controlled retreat that ends the Battle with at least one retinue left — a partial success.
Glossary · Combat Keywords — Defense
TermDefinition
ParryRoll a D10 to attempt to Parry a Strike before an Armor Save. On a 8+ (Improved by Improved Parry), the Strike is Parried and has no further effect.
RiposteIf you Focused a Parry against a Melee Weapon's Strike, you Riposte: your opponent immediately takes a Strike from your melee weapon. You can Riposte a Riposte.
HealIf a to-Save roll fails, roll a D10 & compare it to your Heal value: a result greater than or equal to your Heal value recovers the retinue. Healed Strikes still count toward the Panic check threshold.
EnduringSuccessfully Healed Strikes don't count toward the Panic check threshold.
AwkwardWhile equipped with this weapon during a skirmish, you cannot Parry, and so cannot Riposte.
ShieldedA cumulative -1 penalty to the Strike roll (to a maximum of 10+). Sources: a shield's -1 to Strike.
PlanishingA Focused Save succeeds, regardless of AP.
Glossary · Combat Keywords — Offense
TermDefinition
DeadlyOn a Focused Strike: that strike's AP is increased by 5, and the defender may Parry or Heal only with a Focused roll.
CleaveOn a Focused Strike: roll one extra Strike die at your modified to-Strike.
PoisonWhen the Defender receives a Strike and rolls a Focused Save, it fails; the resulting wound may only be Healed with a Focused Heal.
Destroy ShieldOn a Focused Strike: the target loses its Shield attributes for the rest of the Battle.
Unstoppable-2 to the defender's Parry roll, to a maximum of 10+).
SerratedA cumulative -2 penalty to the defender's Heal roll.
Glossary · Combat Keywords — Handling
TermDefinition
EquippedThe weapon chosen for the Skirmish.
SteadyInitiative cannot be reduced by Tactics.
UnwieldyInitiative cannot be improved by Tactics.
NimbleGain +1 Initiative in the first Skirmish of each Battle.
DrilledDoes not lose Endurance in the first Skirmish of each Battle.
2HCannot use a Shield: while a 2H weapon is Equipped, your Shield gives nothing that Skirmish (no Save bonus, Initiative or keywords).
Dual WieldA failed Strike is rerolled once; the rerolled Strike can be Focused. Dual Wield confers Two-Handed. You cannot reroll successful Strikes.
Dual-equipCarry two weapons at once (e.g. melee + ranged). Granted by the Tiltyard.
One-ShotMust be Equipped in the first Skirmish of a Battle, and can't be Equipped after it. Requires a Tiltyard.
Glossary · Combat Keywords — Immune / Negate
TermDefinition
Immune [keyword]Cancels that keyword as it applies to you (e.g. Immune Unwieldy, Strain, Destroy Shield).
Negate PlanishingIgnores Planishing: this weapon's AP can reduce the target's Save beyond 10+ (to auto-fail).
Negate RiposteThe target's Parry can never Riposte this weapon's Strikes (a natural 10 Parry still cancels the Strike, but no counter-Strike follows).
