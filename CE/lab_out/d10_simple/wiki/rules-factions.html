Factions

FactionFeelDifficultyStrengthMechanic
Siege SpecialistOffensive, ArrogantLowMediumYou begin the game with a Siege Works (no Ward, Natural). When a player builds a Citadel, you must Siege it as an Edict.
HorsemastersFastLowMediumYou begin the game with a Stable (no Ward, Natural).
Friends in Low PlacesScrappyLowLowYou begin the game with a Smuggler's Nook (no Ward, Natural). Each turn, you may control one Bandit Camp outside your Territory. When a Bandit Camp is generated, you may place it in your Outlaw Country instead.
The MonarchSovereign VoiceLowMediumEach turn, you choose every Council Envoy's Domain. Every Envoy you send has Authority −1, except in Spring.
Sea LanesPirate LifeLowMediumOther players can't perform Intercept Caravan on you. You begin the game with a Shipyard (no Ward, Natural).
Royal MarriageDiplomatic, InescapableLowMediumOnce per game, you may join an Alliance without unanimous consent, or form one with a player who isn't in an Alliance. While in that Alliance, each member gains Faith +1 and +1 Influence per turn.
Patient CourtPatient & ReservedLowMediumYour unspent Influence isn't discarded in the Rest Phase. While the Era is Zenith, you may spend up to 5 Influence per Envoy, regardless of your Domain Standing.
The DeciderCold SteelLowMediumIgnore the innate Doubt and Influence modifiers for being At War.
Prosperity For AllEgalitarianLowMediumYou begin the game with an Inn (no Ward, Natural).
Silk RoadRich TraderLowLowYou begin the game with an Artisan Workshop (no Ward, Natural). You can't refuse a Trade Agreement.
Independent, but AmbitiousIndependentLowLowYou Abstain on every vote on other players' Envoys, and other players must spend 2 Influence to affect your Envoys by 1. Your Personal Envoys have Authority +1, except in Spring. You can't vote on Council Envoys.
Knowledge is PowerKnowledge is PowerLowMediumEach turn, gain +1 Influence for every 3 Holdings you have.
Fortress of FaithPiously ResoluteLowMediumWhile your Public Order is 3 or higher, Lay Siege actions targeting Settlements you Control have Siege Timer +2.
ScavengersScavengerLowMediumYou collect the Spoils of War whether you win or lose a Battle, and may also recoup the cost of your Retinues lost as Casualties in that Battle. When you perform the Repair action, Extort 2000 from the taret that performed the Raze action.
UnimpeachableDefensive, ImpenetrableLowMediumYou begin the game with a Citadel (no Ward, Natural). You (and your Alliance) can't perform Declare War or Sacred War.
Heart of TradeTrade DependentLowHighYou begin the game with Stone Roads (no upkeep, can't be Razed). No player may trade unless they first hold a Trade Agreement with you, or are At War with you. When another player becomes eligible, you must attempt once to Sign a Trade Agreement with them. You can't perform the End Treaty action.
SacrosanctPure and DefensiveLowHighWhen a player Declares War on you, that player and their allies gain Doubt +1.
TunnellersMountain PassersLowLowYou treat mountain Territory as grassland for movement, and begin the game with a Mine Raw-Material Holding (no Ward, Natural).
MartyrdomUnconvincedLowLowYour Armies' Morale can't be modified beyond 10+, but still suffer −2 per Fatigue Token. Gain Faith +1 for every player you're At War with, and Faith +1 in every Battle where you lose 20 or more Retinues (win or lose). Your Armies have Immune War Weariness.
Peaceful & InventivePure EconomyLowHighYou can't Declare War, enter Military Alliances, perform Cunning actions, or build Holdings that require Prowess or Cunning Standing. Gain 100 gold per total Craft level across all active sources.
DanegeldAggressive, ParasiticLowMediumWhile At War, your Armies pay no upkeep in Territory your at-war player controls. You don't gain Speed −1 in Winter.
Highlander WayOathkeepersMediumHighEnemy Armies have Speed −2 in your Province. You ignore all terrain Speed modifiers and may trade without Dirt Roads. You must accept the first Non-Aggression Pact each player or Alliance offers you. If that player later joins an Alliance, this is considered satisfied for that Alliance.
Prophets of WarSacred War Without EndMediumHighYou may Sacred War at Rising Piety instead of Sovereign Piety, with no cap on active Sacred Wars. Each player gains Doubt +1 per Sacred War you're on. You can't perform Convert actions.
Burning CrossCrusader FeelMediumHighYou begin the game with a Sacred Preceptory (no Ward, Natural). When you Declared War via Sacred War, that player takes a Panic Check at the start of every Battle, after lines are formed. During Envoy Declaration, if you don't have an Army of 25 Knight's Templars, you must send a Prowess Envoy; if it passes, you must muster an Army until it holds 25 Oathsworn Retinues.
PalatineStrength Through DiplomacyMediumMediumEach player allied with you has Authority +1 on their Envoys. Each turn, you may spend 1 of an ally's Influence, targeting your own Envoys if you wish.
Arts & Humanities / Hearts & MindsCivic Soft PowerMediumMediumYou can't pursue Craft Holdings. Your Civic Holdings have Craft +1 and cost no Upkeep. You must accept a Peace Treaty if one is offered.
Mercantile PactPeace Through TradeMediumMediumWhen a player Declares War on you, each player who holds a Trade Agreement with you must either Declare War back or End that Trade Agreement.
InexorableUndeadMediumLowYour Armies always have Unwieldy and can't gain Immune Unwieldy; they also have Heal +1, Speed −1, and Immune Panic. Your Public Order can't exceed 1.
Integrated ArmsMercantile MilitaryMediumMediumWhen you perform a Muster action, you may muster Retinues and equipment from your Trade Partners' Holdings, paying their cost.
Friends in High PlacesGenerous SponsorMediumMediumAt the start of the turn, a player who supported an Envoy you sent that passed gains Faith +1; if that Envoy was endorsed, they Extort 500 gold per Era.
PestilenceSicklyHighLowEach bordering player gains Doubt +1. Your Settlements have Reach +1, and all your Retinues have Poison. You can't pursue an Apothecary, Infirmary, or Hospitaller.
The Highest BidderAlways for SaleHighLowYou can't Declare War and can't be the target of a Declare War action. Each turn, you must sign the Military or Defensive Alliance offered by the highest-bidding player, and must be paid each turn. To overturn your current Alliance, a player must pay more than the prior agreement. You can't sign or end Alliances via a Diplomacy action.
Masters of DuplicityTwo-FacedHighHighYou may hold any number of Treaties at once with any number of players, even contradictory ones. Your Treaties are never automatically canceled or voided by game events. When something would normally force a Treaty to cancel, you choose which obligation to honor and keep all your other Treaties intact. Other players may still End Treaty with you.
MimicShifting IdentityHighHighAt the start of each turn, choose another player's Faction; you have that Faction this turn. You can't choose that same Faction next turn.
RecklessAggressive, DestructiveHighMediumYour Cunning Envoys have Authority +2 when targeting a player with Public Order above 0. You begin the game with a Secret Cellar (no Ward, Natural). You can't be in an Alliance and can't perform Diplomacy actions. A Prowess Envoy you send can't fail — if it would be condemned, it passes instead.
OccupyMobile OccupierHighMediumYou start with an additional Army; all your Armies begin inside your Settlements. While you're outside a Settlement, you don't control it — to collect taxes or use its Holdings, end your turn inside it. To Occupy a Settlement, begin a Battle adjacent to it as if Sieging, but instead Battle the Garrison and any Armies at War with you or sharing its owner; if you win (or there's no force to fight), move inside. In the Upkeep Phase, pay upkeep only on equipment. You count as having all Infrastructure of your Occupied Settlements, and always count as 3 Trading Holdings. When you leave a Settlement, you can't re-enter it until you Occupy another Settlement and sign a Trade Agreement.
Wandering NomadsNomadsHighMediumYour Armies function as Settlements — a Band, Tribe, or Horde, upgraded accordingly — count as Settlements with Reach 3, and each has a Muster Field. You may have one additional Army per Era, starting with two. Your Armies don't require troops to exist and can't be besieged. You can't build Infrastructure, but may build Holdings; Raw Material Holdings within Reach are always active and take no Settlement Ward. You may trade so long as your border touches another player's. You may begin a new Army adjacent to any existing Army with a Charter Settlement or Muster Army action. When an Army is destroyed, so is its Settlement. Nomads pay no upkeep.
The Great ArbiterKing of the CastleHighHighThe Duke begins the game trading with every player under a Non-Aggression Pact. The Duke can't join Alliances, perform Cunning actions, or Declare War. When a player Declares War on the Duke, that player's Trade Agreements, Non-Aggression Pacts, and Defensive Alliances are immediately and permanently voided (their Military Alliance stays intact), and every other player simultaneously Declares War on that player and their Military Alliance; none of these can be reinstated while At War with the Duke. The Duke sees all private actions, resolves player actions, and manages Bandit Mechanics. If the Duke is eliminated, the game ends. The Duke may optionally choose a Faction.
Wise & SuspiciousCautious QuickfightersLowMediumGain Doubt +1. While your Public Order is 1 or higher, your Armies have +1 Initiative, Steady, and Speed +1. You can't be in an Alliance.
Archery is a Way of LifeSkilled VolleysLowMediumYou begin the game with a Fletchery (no Ward, Natural). Your Armies must always equip a ranged weapon, and Ranged Weapons have +1 to Strike. Your Armies can't equip full plate or articulated gothic plate, can't use shields, and can't build a Preceptory of Oathsworn.
