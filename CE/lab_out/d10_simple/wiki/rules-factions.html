Siege SpecialistsOffensive, ArrogantLowMediumYou begin the game with a Siege Works (no Ward, Natural).
HorsemastersFastLowMediumYou begin the game with a Stable (no Ward, Natural).
Friends in Low PlacesScrappyHighHighYou begin the game with a Smuggler's Nook (no Ward, Natural). At the Start of the Empire Phase, you may control one Bandit Camp outside your Territory. When a Bandit Camp is spawned, you may place it in your Outlaw Country instead. You cannot Build Holdings which require Piety or Prowess Standing.
The MonarchSovereign VoiceLowMediumEach Council Phase, you choose every Council Envoy's Domain. You abstain every Personal Envoy.
Uncharted Sea LanesPirate LifeLowMediumOther players can't perform Intercept Caravan on you. You begin the game with a Shipyard (no Ward, Natural).
Royal MarriageDiplomatic, InescapableLowMediumOnce per game, you may join an Alliance without unanimous consent, or form one with a player who isn't in an Alliance. While in that Alliance, each member gains Faith +1 and Influence +1 each Empire Phase.
Patient CourtPatient & ReservedLowMediumYour unspent Influence isn't discarded in the Rest Phase. While the Era is Zenith, your Max Influence is 5 on every Envoy.
The DeciderCold SteelLowMediumIgnore the innate Doubt and Influence modifiers for being At War.
Prosperity For AllEgalitarianLowMediumYou begin the game with an Inn (no Ward, Natural).
Luxury TradesRich TraderLowLowYou begin the game with an Artisan Workshop (no Ward, Natural). You can't refuse a Trade Agreement.
Independent, but AmbitiousIndependentLowLowYou Abstain on every vote on other players' Envoys, and other players must spend 2 Influence to affect your Envoys by 1. Your Personal Envoys have Authority +1. You can't vote nor send Council Envoys.
Knowledge is PowerKnowledge is PowerLowMediumInfluence +1 per 3 Holdings you have.
Fortress of FaithPiously ResoluteLowMediumWhile your Public Order is 4 or higher, Lay Siege actions targeting you have Siege Timer +3.
ScavengersScavengerLowMediumYou collect the Spoils of War whether you win or lose a Battle. When a Raze action targets you, Extort 2000 from the attacker.
UnimpeachableDefensive, ImpenetrableLowMediumYou begin the game with a Citadel (no Ward, Natural). You (and your Alliance) can't perform Declare War or Sacred War actions.
Heart of TradeTrade DependentLowHighYou begin the game with Stone Roads (no upkeep, can't be Razed). No player may trade unless they first hold a Trade Agreement with you, or are At War with you. When another player becomes eligible, you must attempt once to Sign a Trade Agreement with them. You can't perform the End Treaty action.
SacrosanctPure and DefensiveLowHighWhen a player Declares War on you, that player and their allies gain Doubt +5.
TunnellersMountain PassersLowLowYou treat mountain Territory as grassland for movement, and begin the game with a Mine Raw-Material Holding (no Ward, Natural).
MartyrdomUnconvincedLowLowYour Armies' Morale can't be modified beyond 10+, but still suffer −2 per Fatigue Token. Gain Faith +1 for every player you're At War with each Empire Phase, and Faith +1 in every Battle where you lose 20 or more Retinues (win or lose). You gain Immune War Weariness.
Peaceful & InventivePure EconomyLowHighYou can't Declare War, enter Military Alliances, perform Cunning actions, or build Holdings that require Prowess or Cunning Standing. Gain 100 gold per total Craft level across all active sources.
PillagersAggressive, ParasiticLowMediumWhile At War, your Armies within reach of at-war Settlements Extort their upkeep from that Settlement's owner. You cannot join an alliance.
Terrain NegotiatorsOathkeepersMediumHighEnemy Armies have Speed −2 in your Province. You ignore all negative terrain Speed modifiers and may trade without Dirt Roads. You must accept the first Non-Aggression Pact each player or Alliance offers you. If that player later joins an Alliance, this is considered satisfied for that Alliance.
Prophets of WarSacred War Without EndMediumHighYou may Sacred War at Rising Piety instead of Sovereign Piety, with no cap on active Sacred Wars. Each other player gains Doubt +1 each Empire Phase per Sacred War you're on. You can't perform Convert or Cunning actions.
Burning CrossCrusader FeelMediumHighYou begin the game with a Sacred Preceptory (no Ward, Natural). When you Declared War via Sacred War, that player takes a Panic Check at the start of every Battle, after lines are formed. During Envoy Declaration, if you don't have an Army of 25 Oathsworn, you must send a Prowess Envoy; if it passes, you must muster an Army until it holds 25 Oathsworn Retinues.
PalatineStrength Through DiplomacyMediumMediumEach player allied with you has Authority +1 on their Personal Envoys. Once per Envoy Phase, you may spend 1 of an ally's Influence, targeting your own Envoys if you wish.
Arts & Humanities / Hearts & MindsCivic Soft PowerMediumMediumYou can't build Industry Holdings. Your Holdings which require Piety have Craft +2 and cost no Upkeep. You must accept a Peace Treaty if one is offered.
Mercantile PactPeace Through TradeMediumMediumPlayers who sign a Trade Agreement with you are considered to be in a Defensive Alliance with you. You cannot perform the Declare War action.
InexorableUndeadMediumLowYour Armies always have Unwieldy; they also gain Heal 8, Speed −1, and Immune Panic. Your Public Order can't exceed 1.
Integrated ArmsMercantile MilitaryMediumMediumWhen you perform a Muster action, you may muster Retinues and equipment from your Trade Partners' Holdings.
Friends in High PlacesGenerous SponsorMediumMediumEach Empire Phase, a player who supported a Personal Envoy you sent that passed gains Faith +1; if that Personal Envoy was endorsed, they Extort 500 gold from you.
PestilenceSicklyHighLowEach bordering player gains Doubt +1 each Empire Phase. Your Settlements have Reach +2, and all your Retinues have Poison. You can't build an Apothecary, Infirmary, or Hospitaller.
The Highest BidderAlways for SaleHighLowYou can't Declare War and can't be the target of a Declare War action. Each Empire Phase, you must sign the Military or Defensive Alliance offered by the highest-bidding player, and must be paid each Empire Phase. To overturn your current Alliance, a player must pay more than the prior agreement. You can't sign or end Alliances via a Diplomacy action.
Masters of DuplicityTwo-FacedHighHighYou may hold any number of Treaties at once with any number of players, even contradictory ones. Your Treaties are never automatically canceled or voided by game events. When something would normally force a Treaty to cancel, you choose which obligation to honor and keep all your Treaties intact. Other players may still End Treaty with you.
MimicShifting IdentityHighHighAt the Start of the Empire Phase, choose another player's Heritage; you have that Heritage's mechanic this turn. You can't choose that same Heritage next turn.
Double SpeakSubterfuge, DeceptionHighHighYour Diplomacy Envoys start at Authority equal to your Cunning rank, and your Max Influence on Diplomacy Envoys is your Cunning rank. Whenever your Personal Diplomacy Envoy passes or is endorsed, you may instead perform a Cunning action. You can't perform Declare War or Sacred War actions, and you can't be a member of an Alliance.
RecklessAggressive, DestructiveHighHighAuthority +2 on your Cunning actions targeting a player with Public Order 2 or higher. You begin the game with a Secret Cellar (no Ward, Natural). You can't be in an Alliance or perform Diplomacy actions. A Prowess Envoy you send can't fail or be condemned & will instead pass. You cannot put Domain Points into Piety, nor have more than 3 in Industry.
OccupyMobile OccupierHighMediumYou start with an additional Army; all your Armies begin inside your Settlements. While you're outside a Settlement, you don't control it — to collect taxes or use its Holdings, end your turn inside it. To Occupy a Settlement, begin a Battle adjacent to it as if Sieging, but instead Battle the Garrison and any Armies at War with you or sharing its owner; if you win (or there's no force to fight), move inside. In the Upkeep Phase, you only pay upkeep on your Armies. You count as having all Infrastructure of your Occupied Settlements, and always count as having Craft 5.
Wandering NomadsNomadsHighMediumYour Armies function as Settlements — a Band, Tribe, or Horde, upgraded accordingly — count as Settlements with Reach 3, and each has a Muster Field. You may have one additional Army per Era, starting with two. Your Armies don't require troops to exist and can't be besieged. You can't build Infrastructure, but may build Holdings; Raw Material Holdings within your Reach are always active and take no Settlement Ward. You may trade so long as your border touches another player's. You may begin a new Army adjacent to any existing Army with a Settle or Muster action. When an Army is destroyed, so is its Settlement. Nomads pay no upkeep.
The DukeKing of the CastleHighHighThe Duke begins the game trading with every player under a Non-Aggression Pact. The Duke can't join Alliances, perform Cunning actions, or Declare War. When a player Declares War on the Duke, that player's Trade Agreements, Non-Aggression Pacts, and Defensive Alliances are immediately and permanently voided (their Military Alliance stays intact), and every other player simultaneously Declares War on that player and their Military Alliance; none of these can be reinstated while At War with the Duke. The Duke sees all private actions, resolves player actions, and manages Bandit Mechanics. If the Duke is eliminated, the game ends. The Duke may optionally choose a Heritage.
Wise & SuspiciousCautious QuickfightersLowMediumGain Doubt +1 each Empire Phase. While your Public Order is 1 or higher, your Armies gain Initiative +1, Steady, and Speed +1. You can't be in an Alliance.
Archery is a Way of LifeSkilled VolleysLowMediumYou begin the game with a Fletchery (no Ward, Natural). Your Armies must always have a ranged weapon, and Ranged Weapons gain +1 to Strike. Your Armies can't equip full plate, gothic plate, or shields.
