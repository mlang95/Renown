Siege Warfare

  • When you Lay Siege during the Envoy Phase, track the relevant Siege Timer(s).
  • When a Siege Timer reaches 0, resolve the Siege before the Rest Phase: negotiate, then Battle if needed, then Capture or Sack.

Siege Timer

A Siege Timer is the sum of the besieged Settlement's defenses minus the attacker's Siege effects, to a minimum of 1 (no maximum). Every source below stacks — Wooden Walls and Stone Walls both count.

Beginning a Siege

  • End a Move action with your Army adjacent to another player's Settlement you're At War with.
  • Your Army must stay there for the Siege Timer and can't take other actions.
  • If you're attacked while sieging and you Fall Back, the Siege is broken and the Siege Timer is removed from play.
  • While a Settlement is besieged: it can't count Holdings toward Craft X; its Armies gain Strained and can't perform Move actions except to Sally Forth; Timers targeting the Settlement don't count down; and Industry actions can't target it.

Resolving a Siege

When a Siege Timer reaches 0:

  • If you haven't negotiated yet, negotiate now.
  • If negotiation fails and there's an Army or Garrison inside, Battle in the Battle Phase.
  • If the besieging player wins, or there's no eligible Army or Garrison, choose one:
    • Capture: set a Capture Timer 1. When it resolves, the Settlement comes under your Control — it receives your active Infrastructure effects, and you gain its Holdings, tax income, and Reach.
    • Sack: Raze the top-most Holding of each Ward in the Settlement, Extort 2000 per Settlement tier; reduce the Settlement by 1 tier. Its controller removes all Holdings from one Settlement Ward of the sacking player's choice, then removes that Ward from play. Set a Sack Timer 2 — you can't Lay Siege again until it resolves. Your Army is Strained and can't perform Move actions until the Sack Timer resolves.
Glossary · War
TermDefinition
At WarTwo players At War can't trade, and may Battle, Lay Siege on and Pillage each other.
Lay SiegeA Move that ends Adjacent to an at-war Settlement, starting its Siege Timer. See 11.2.4 (Beginning a Siege).
SiegeAn Army holding Adjacent to an at-war Settlement while a Siege Timer counts down to Capture or Sack it. See 11.2 (Siege Warfare).
CaptureWhen a Siege resolves in your favor: set a Capture Timer; when it resolves, the Settlement comes under your Control. See 11.2.5 (Resolving a Siege).
SackWhen a Siege resolves in your favor: Damage the top Holding of each Ward, Extort 2000 per Settlement tier, reduce it one tier and remove one Ward. See 11.2.5 (Resolving a Siege).
PillageA Prowess action: while your Army stays within Reach of an at-war Settlement, it Extorts half of that Settlement's Holding income each Empire Phase, until it Battles, Lays Siege or Marches. See 5.4 (Prowess Actions).
Sally ForthWhile a Settlement you control is besieged and you have an Army or Garrison inside it, you may Sally Forth: Battle in the Battle Phase without performing an action.
