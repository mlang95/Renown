Trade & Income

Who Can Trade

Who can trade: a player needs a signed Trade Agreement and either active Dirt Roads or a Can Trade effect (e.g. Harbor).

Craft X: Craft Holdings count toward how much income your Trade Agreements generate — any effect that grants Craft +X.

Trade income: for each active Trade Agreement, both players gain gold equal to 100 × the Host's Craft X.

When income flows:

  • When you sign a Trade Agreement: the player who did not perform the Sign Treaty action gets trade income the next time they're Host. Afterwards, both get trade income each turn either of them is Host.
  • When you end a Trade Agreement: the player who ended it gets trade income the next time they're Host, then trading ceases.
RuleValue
Income per Craft100
RequirementsA signed Trade Agreement, and each player has active Dirt Roads or a Can Trade effect (e.g. Harbor, Shipyard)
No-trade SeasonSpring

Income Types

  • Taxes: each Winter, in the Yields & Expenditures step of the Empire Phase, gain tax income from each of your Settlements by tier, after modifiers.
  • Trade: see above.
  • Holdings: gain Holding income in the Yields & Expenditures step of the Empire Phase.
