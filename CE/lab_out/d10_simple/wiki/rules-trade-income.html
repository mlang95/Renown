Trade & Income

Who Can Trade

Who can trade: a player needs a signed Trade Agreement and either active Dirt Roads or a Can Trade effect (e.g. Harbor).

Craft X: Craft Holdings count toward how much income your Trade Agreements generate — any effect that grants Craft +X.

Trade income: for each active Trade Agreement, both players gain gold equal to 100 × the Host's Craft X.

When income flows:

  • When you sign a Trade Agreement: the player who did not perform the Sign Treaty action gets trade income the next time they're Host. Afterwards, both get trade income each turn either of them is Host.
  • When you end a Trade Agreement: the player who ended it gets trade income the next time they're Host, then trading ceases.
RuleValue
Income per Craft100
RequirementsA signed Trade Agreement, and each player has active Dirt Roads or a Can Trade effect (e.g. Harbor, Shipyard)
No-trade SeasonSpring

Income Types

  • Taxes: each Winter, in the Income & Upkeep step of the Empire Phase, gain tax income from each of your Settlements by tier, after modifiers.
  • Trade: see above.
  • Holdings: gain Holding income in the Income & Upkeep step of the Empire Phase.

Trade PartnerA player you have a Trade Agreement with and can trade with. See 9.3.1 (Who Can Trade).
CraftYour trade value, from every effect that gives Craft +X. See 9.3.1 (Who Can Trade).
Can TradeYou may take part in trade without active Dirt Roads (you still need a signed Trade Agreement). See 9.3.1 (Who Can Trade).
Public OrderA track from −10 to 10, adjusted each turn by Faith minus Doubt; its band applies cumulative effects (see the Public Order table). See 9.1 (Public Order).
Faith XGain X Faith: each Faith raises your Public Order track by 1 when resolved. See 9.1 (Public Order).
Doubt XGain X Doubt: each Doubt lowers your Public Order track by 1 when resolved. See 9.1 (Public Order).
Extort XTake X from the stated source: the gold goes to you instead of its owner.
Recoup XUndo up to X of the cost you paid (refund gold, or remove Doubt gained).
War WearinessDoubt 1 each Empire Phase per consecutive Battle you've lost. See 9.1.5 (Innate Public Order Modifiers).
