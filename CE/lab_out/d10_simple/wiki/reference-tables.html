Bandits

TermRule
Bandit Domain ValueFor every 5 Retinues in the Bandit Camp or Army, the Bandit Camp has +2 Cunning and +2 Prowess.
Bandit CampA collection of Bandits in Outlaw Country, starting with 10 Retinues and 2000 gold.
Bandit GrowthBandit Camps gain (1/2/3/4) Retinues a turn, based on the Era of the Realm.
Bandit ArmyA Bandit Camp at 25 Retinues sends out a Bandit Army of 25; the camp stays, and the Army returns to it after each Battle to re-muster. Performs Move actions toward the closest Settlement or Army, Pillaging or Skirmishing if possible, in addition to Bandit Cunning Mechanics. Bandit Armies cannot exceed 25
Spawn a Bandit CampEach Spring, every player gains a Bandit Camp.
