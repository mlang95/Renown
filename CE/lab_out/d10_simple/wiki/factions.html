Factions

Archery is a Way of Life
You begin the game with a Fletchery (no Ward, Natural). Your Armies must always have a ranged weapon, and Ranged Weapons gain +1 to Strike. Your Armies can't equip full plate, articulated gothic plate, or shields.
Arts & Humanities / Hearts & Minds
You can't build Commerce Holdings. Your Works & Devotion Holdings have Craft +1 and cost no Upkeep. You must accept a Peace Treaty if one is offered.
Burning Cross
You begin the game with a Sacred Preceptory (no Ward, Natural). When you Declared War via Sacred War, that player takes a Panic Check at the start of every Battle, after lines are formed. During Envoy Declaration, if you don't have an Army of 25 Knight's Templars, you must send a Prowess Envoy; if it passes, you must muster an Army until it holds 25 Oathsworn Retinues.
Danegeld
While At War, your Armies within reach of at-war Settlements Extort their upkeep from that settlement's controller. You cannot join an alliance.
Fortress of Faith
While your Public Order is 3 or higher, Lay Siege actions targeting Settlements you Control have Siege Timer +3.
Friends in High Places
At the start of the turn, a player who supported an Envoy you sent that passed gains Faith +1; if that Envoy was endorsed, they Extort 500 gold from you.
Friends in Low Places
You begin the game with a Smuggler's Nook (no Ward, Natural). Each turn, you may control one Bandit Camp outside your Territory. When a Bandit Camp is generated, you may place it in your Outlaw Country instead.
Heart of Trade
You begin the game with Stone Roads (no upkeep, can't be Razed). No player may trade unless they first hold a Trade Agreement with you, or are At War with you. When another player becomes eligible, you must attempt once to Sign a Trade Agreement with them. You can't perform the End Treaty action.
Highlander Way
Enemy Armies have Speed −2 in your Province. You ignore all negative terrain Speed modifiers and may trade without Dirt Roads. You must accept the first Non-Aggression Pact each player or Alliance offers you. If that player later joins an Alliance, this is considered satisfied for that Alliance.
Horsemasters
You begin the game with a Stable (no Ward, Natural).
Independent, but Ambitious
You Abstain on every vote on other players' Envoys, and other players must spend 2 Influence to affect your Envoys by 1. Your Personal Envoys have Authority +1. You can't vote nor send Council Envoys.
Inexorable
Your Armies always have Unwieldy; they also gain Heal 8, Speed −1, and Immune Panic. Your Public Order can't exceed 1.
Integrated Arms
When you perform a Muster action, you may muster Retinues and equipment from your Trade Partners' Holdings.
Knowledge is Power
Each turn, gain +1 Influence for every 3 Holdings you have.
Martyrdom
Your Armies' Morale can't be modified beyond 10+, but still suffer −2 per Fatigue Token. Gain Faith +1 for every player you're At War with per Empire Phase, and Faith +1 in every Battle where you lose 20 or more Retinues (win or lose). You gain Immune War Weariness.
Masters of Duplicity
You may hold any number of Treaties at once with any number of players, even contradictory ones. Your Treaties are never automatically canceled or voided by game events. When something would normally force a Treaty to cancel, you choose which obligation to honor and keep all your Treaties intact. Other players may still End Treaty with you.
Mercantile Pact
Players who sign a Trade Agreement with you are considered to be in a Defensive Alliance with you. You cannot perform the Declare War action.
Mimic
At the start of each turn, choose another player's Faction; you have that Faction's mechanic this turn. You can't choose that same Faction next turn.
Occupy
You start with an additional Army; all your Armies begin inside your Settlements. While you're outside a Settlement, you don't control it — to collect taxes or use its Holdings, end your turn inside it. To Occupy a Settlement, begin a Battle adjacent to it as if Sieging, but instead Battle the Garrison and any Armies at War with you or sharing its owner; if you win (or there's no force to fight), move inside. In the Upkeep Phase, you only pay upkeep on your armies. You count as having all Infrastructure of your Occupied Settlements, and always count as having Craft 5.
Palatine
Each player allied with you has Authority +1 on their Envoys. Each turn, you may spend 1 of an ally's Influence, targeting your own Envoys if you wish.
Patient Court
Your unspent Influence isn't discarded in the Rest Phase. While the Era is Zenith, you may spend up to 5 Influence per Envoy while voting, regardless of your Domain Standing.
Peaceful & Inventive
You can't Declare War, enter Military Alliances, perform Cunning actions, or build Holdings that require Prowess or Cunning Standing. Gain 100 gold per total Craft level across all active sources.
Pestilence
Each bordering player gains Doubt +1 per Empire Phase. Your Settlements have Reach +2, and all your Retinues have Poison. You can't build an Apothecary, Infirmary, or Hospitaller.
Prophets of War
You may Sacred War at Rising Piety instead of Sovereign Piety, with no cap on active Sacred Wars. Each player gains Doubt +1 per Empire Phase per Sacred War you're on. You can't perform Convert or Cunning actions.
Prosperity For All
You begin the game with an Inn (no Ward, Natural).
Reckless
Your Cunning Envoys have Authority +2 when targeting a player with Public Order greater than 0. You begin the game with a Secret Cellar (no Ward, Natural). You can't be in an Alliance and can't perform Diplomacy actions. A Prowess Envoy you send can't fail or be condemned & will instead pass.
Royal Marriage
Once per game, you may join an Alliance without unanimous consent, or form one with a player who isn't in an Alliance. While in that Alliance, each member gains Faith +1 and +1 Influence per turn.
Sacrosanct
When a player Declares War on you, that player and their allies gain Doubt +5.
Scavengers
You collect the Spoils of War whether you win or lose a Battle. When you are the target of a Raze action, Extort 2000 from the target that performed the Raze action.
Sea Lanes
Other players can't perform Intercept Caravan on you. You begin the game with a Shipyard (no Ward, Natural).
Siege Specialist
You begin the game with a Siege Works (no Ward, Natural). When a player builds a Citadel, you must Siege it as an Edict.
Silk Road
You begin the game with an Artisan Workshop (no Ward, Natural). You can't refuse a Trade Agreement.
The Decider
Ignore the innate Doubt and Influence modifiers for being At War.
The Great Arbiter
The Duke begins the game trading with every player under a Non-Aggression Pact. The Duke can't join Alliances, perform Cunning actions, or Declare War. When a player Declares War on the Duke, that player's Trade Agreements, Non-Aggression Pacts, and Defensive Alliances are immediately and permanently voided (their Military Alliance stays intact), and every other player simultaneously Declares War on that player and their Military Alliance; none of these can be reinstated while At War with the Duke. The Duke sees all private actions, resolves player actions, and manages Bandit Mechanics. If the Duke is eliminated, the game ends. The Duke may optionally choose a Faction.
The Highest Bidder
You can't Declare War and can't be the target of a Declare War action. Each turn, you must sign the Military or Defensive Alliance offered by the highest-bidding player, and must be paid each turn. To overturn your current Alliance, a player must pay more than the prior agreement. You can't sign or end Alliances via a Diplomacy action.
The Monarch
Each turn, you choose every Council Envoy's Domain. You abstain every Personal Envoy.
Tunnellers
You treat mountain Territory as grassland for movement, and begin the game with a Mine Raw-Material Holding (no Ward, Natural).
Unimpeachable
You begin the game with a Citadel (no Ward, Natural). You (and your Alliance) can't perform Declare War or Sacred War actions.
Wandering Nomads
Your Armies function as Settlements — a Band, Tribe, or Horde, upgraded accordingly — count as Settlements with Reach 3, and each has a Muster Field. You may have one additional Army per Era, starting with two. Your Armies don't require troops to exist and can't be besieged. You can't build Infrastructure, but may build Holdings; Raw Material Holdings within Reach are always active and take no Settlement Ward. You may trade so long as your border touches another player's. You may begin a new Army adjacent to any existing Army with a Charter Settlement or Muster Army action. When an Army is destroyed, so is its Settlement. Nomads pay no upkeep.
Wise & Suspicious
Gain Doubt +1 per Empire Phase. While your Public Order is 1 or higher, your Armies gain +1 Initiative, Steady, and Speed +1. You can't be in an Alliance.
