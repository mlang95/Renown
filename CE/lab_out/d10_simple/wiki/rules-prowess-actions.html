MusterNone—Choose one: Raise — if you have fewer Armies than your Era allows, place a new Army within a Settlement under your Control; or Reinforce — choose an Army you control. The Army must be within range 2 of a Settlement you Control with an active Muster Field, and at least Range 2 from any non-allied Army unless it is within Range 0 of a Settlement you Control. Choose how many Retinues to recruit (an Army can't exceed 25) and set a Muster Timer equal to that number ÷ your combined muster limit, rounded up. Each turn the Muster Timer counts down, the Army gains Retinues up to your combined muster limit and you begin paying their upkeep. You may change the Army's Retinue type and equipment while an army has an active Muster Timer. — Combined muster limit: the total muster limit of your Settlements whose Reach includes the Army's Territory.; While a Settlement is besieged, its muster limit is 0; an active Muster Timer doesn't count down and may be cancelled at any time.Perform a Move action.

If the target refuses, or later fails to meet the terms, immediately Declare War on them. — Terms may include gold, Settlements, Territory, Treaties, or promises. A promise is unenforceable, but breaking one counts as failing the terms.Perform a Move action.

Rule: Commanding Presence

An Army can't move within Range 2 of a non-allied Army — its Presence — unless that Move performs a Battle against it. Settlements have Presence while their owner has a Garrison, and Settlement Wards while their owner has a Keep; a Move that performs a Battle or Lay Siege against a Settlement may pass through the Presence of that Settlement and its Wards. Bandit Armies have Presence; Bandit Camps don't. An Army that begins a Move within a non-allied Presence may only move to end outside every Presence (or, if at War, to Battle or Lay Siege); if it can't, it can't move.
