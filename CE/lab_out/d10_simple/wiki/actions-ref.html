Actions 23

Every Envoy action, grouped by Domain. Each is Sent during the Envoy Phase, voted on, and resolved on a pass.

Prowess

ActionCostRequiresEffect (if passes)Endorsed
MoveNone—Move an Army up to its Speed in Territories. Then choose one: March — move up to 2× Speed, lose 1 Endurance, take no other action; Battle — end adjacent to an at-war Army not in a Settlement, then begin a Battle; Lay Siege — end adjacent to an at-war Settlement, then begin a Siege.<br><span class='mut'>Unless the move action was Endorsed, an Army may be the target of only one Move action per turn.; Presence limits where the Army may move (see Rule: Commanding Presence).; While an Army&#x27;s Battle or Siege Timer is active, it can&#x27;t be the target of actions.; When an Army performs the Battle action, it gains Seize the Initiative in that Battle&#x27;s first Skirmish.</span>Perform a Prowess action.
Declare WarNoneRising ProwessChoose a non-allied player you have no NAP or active truce with. You and that player are now At War, and any Trade Agreements between you end. If the target is in a Defensive Alliance, every member is now At War with you.<br><span class='mut'>If you are in a Military Alliance, then at the start of your next turn every member Declares War on the target and sends 1 fewer Envoy that turn.</span>Perform a Prowess action.
PillageNoneRising ProwessChoose an army you control within Reach of an at-war player's Settlement without an active Siege Timer. While that army remains within Reach of that Settlement and does not Lay Siege, Battle, or March, it may Extort half of that Settlement's holding income in the Extort step. A Settlement may be Pillaged once per Empire Phase. Pillage ends if [war ends / army leaves Reach / Settlement is besieged].Perform a Prowess action.
MusterNone—Choose one: Raise — if you have fewer Armies than your Era allows, place a new Army within a Settlement under your Control; or Reinforce — choose an Army you control. The Army must be within range 2 of a Settlement you Control, while you have an active Muster Field, and at least Range 2 from any non-allied Army unless it is within Range 0 of a Settlement you Control. Choose how many Retinues to recruit (an Army can't exceed 25) and set a Muster Timer equal to that number ÷ your combined muster limit, rounded up. Each turn the Muster Timer counts down, the Army gains Retinues up to your combined muster limit and you begin paying their upkeep. You may change the Army's Retinue type and equipment while an army has an active Muster Timer. Reinforcing an Army that already has an active Muster Timer instead hurries it: reduce that Muster Timer by 1, and the Army gains Retinues up to your combined muster limit at once.<br><span class='mut'>Combined muster limit: the total muster limit of your Settlements whose Reach includes the Army&#x27;s Territory.; While a Settlement is besieged, its muster limit is 0; an active Muster Timer doesn&#x27;t count down and may be cancelled at any time.</span>Perform a Prowess action.
Demand TributeNone—Choose a non-allied player you have no NAP with, then negotiate terms. If the target agrees, sign a NAP or Alliance (the target picks among the offered terms). If the target refuses, or later fails to meet the terms, immediately Declare War on them.<br><span class='mut'>Terms may include gold, Settlements, Territory, Treaties, or promises. A promise is unenforceable, but breaking one counts as failing the terms.</span>Perform a Prowess action.
