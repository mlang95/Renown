Glossary

2H
Cannot use a Shield: while a 2H weapon is Equipped, your Shield gives nothing that Skirmish (no Save bonus, Initiative or keywords).
AP
Armor Penetration — a weapon's (negative) modifier to the defender's Save roll; the more negative, the harder to save.
Abstain
Decline to spend Influence on a Vote.
Adjacent
Range 1.
Alliance
A Treaty binding players to mutual support; Military and Defensive Alliances unlock by Era. → in context
Army
Retinues on the map under your Control. Armies Move, Battle and Lay Siege, and pay Supply by Retinue type.
At War
Two players At War can't trade, and may Battle, Lay Siege on and Pillage each other.
Attacker / Defender
The Attacker performed the Move action that caused the Battle or Lay Siege; the other side is the Defender.
Authority X
An automatic change to an Envoy's Authority from a Holding, Infrastructure or Heritage, applied while it's sent and resolved (once the action and target are chosen, if it depends on them).
Awkward
While Equipped with this weapon during a Skirmish, you cannot Parry, and so cannot Riposte.
Bandit
Neutral hostile force; Bandit Camps spawn in Outlaw Country and on low Public Order. → in context
Bankrupt
Insolvent with nothing left to cut: your Treasury goes negative by the debt. You can't spend gold or be Extorted, and Revenue pays the debt first. It ends when your Treasury reaches 0.
Battle
A collection of Skirmishes between two players, continuing until a side's Retinue count is reduced to 0, Routs, or successfully Falls Back, ending the Battle.
Battle Phase
Battles and Sieges begun this turn resolve.
Blocked
The next Move action this Army Performs fails, and then it loses Blocked. (An Army with Immune Blocked never gains it.)
Blunder
At Initiative −2, your to-Strike is set to 10+, before other negative modifiers.
Border
You border a player when your Province is within or Adjacent to theirs.
Break check
Taken by each Fatigued side's Field every Skirmish, just before it gains its Fatigue Token. Roll Morale (5 dice, modified by Fatigue Tokens); failures are Casualties, but a Break check never triggers a Panic check. Unbreakable auto-passes.
Build
Build a Holding, spending its purchase cost and a Settlement Ward (Industry action). → in context
Build Timer
Turns until a Holding, Infrastructure or Settlement finishes building and becomes active.
Can Trade
You may take part in trade without active Dirt Roads (you still need a signed Trade Agreement).
Capture
When a Siege resolves in your favor: set a Capture Timer; when it resolves, the Settlement comes under your Control.
Capture Timer
Turns until a Captured Settlement comes under your Control.
Cast
Cast equipment tier. Weapons: Daggers, Short Sword, Spear; Ranged: Longbow; Armor: Leather; Shield: Targe Shield. Unlocked by: Furnace (weapons), Tannery (armor & shield).
Casualty
A Retinue removed from the Field — from an unsaved Strike or a failed Panic or Break check.
Cleave
On a Focused Strike: roll one extra Strike die at your modified to-Strike.
Condemned
Net Authority −4 or less.
Contested
Territory that is Controlled by more than one player's Settlement(s).
Controlled
Territory that is within Reach of a Player's Settlement(s).
Convert Timer
Turns until a Convert attempt resolves; it fails early if conditions change.
Cost / Pay
Cost to Perform an action, usually in Gold or Doubt.
Council Envoy
A free Envoy of Industry sent in the End of Empire Phase; auto-Abstained, Net Authority floored at 1.
End of Empire Phase
The phase after the Empire Phase: each player sends a free Council Envoy of Industry, which Performs the Era's Council actions. There is no Vote. In Spring it is a Diplomacy step instead (offers between players).
Craft
Your trade value, from every effect that gives Craft +X. For each active Trade Agreement, both players gain 100 × the Host's Craft.
Crude
Crude equipment tier. Weapons: Farm Tool, Cudgel, Pitchfork; Ranged: Hunting Bow; Armor: Padded; Shield: Buckler Shield. Unlocked by: Animal Husbandry (armor & shield).
Damaged
A Holding or Infrastructure hit by Raze, Sack or Bandits: it has no effect and pays no Upkeep until Repaired.
Deadly
On a Focused Strike: that Strike's AP is increased by 5, and the defender may Parry or Heal only with a Focused roll.
Demand Tribute
Coerce gold or concessions from another player (Prowess action). → in context
Destroy Shield
On a Focused Strike: the target loses its Shield attributes for the rest of the Battle.
Diplomacy
Sign Treaty, Negotiate and End Treaty: offers between players in the Envoy Phase's Diplomacy step (in Spring, the End of Empire Phase), not sent as Envoys.
Domain
One of the four Domains: Industry, Prowess, Cunning, Piety. Spend Domain Points to raise its value; the value sets your Standing there. → in context
Domain Point
Gain 1 each Rest Phase; spend to raise a Domain value by 1.
Doubt X
Gain X Doubt: each Doubt lowers your Public Order track by 1 when resolved.
Drilled
Does not lose Endurance in the first Skirmish of each Battle.
Dual Wield
A failed Strike is rerolled once; the rerolled Strike can be Focused. Dual Wield confers Two-Handed. You cannot reroll successful Strikes.
Dual-equip
Carry two weapons at once (e.g. melee + ranged). Granted by the Skirmish Field.
Edict
A scoring achievement. Grants the Realm +1 Renown every time one is achieved. → in context
Empire Phase
The first phase of the turn: Timers, Bandits, Influence and Envoys, income, Supply and Upkeep, then Public Order.
Endorsed
Net Authority 4+.
Endurance
A side's stamina. Each side that fights loses 1 per Skirmish; at 0 it becomes Fatigued.
Enduring
Successfully Healed Strikes don't count toward the Panic check threshold.
Envoy
Your means of acting: declare a Domain and spend 1 Envoy, and if it passes, choose and Perform an action of that Domain. Personal Envoys come from your Era; every player also sends a free Council Envoy.
Envoy Phase
Every player sends their Envoys; they are voted on, then resolved Domain by Domain.
Equipped
The weapon chosen for the Skirmish.
Era
The stage of the game, set by Renown: Founding, Ascension, Eminence, Zenith. It sets Envoys, Influence and other limits. → in context
Expand
Raise a Settlement one tier: Village to Town, Town to City, City to Metropolis (Industry action). → in context
Extort X
Take X from the stated source: the gold goes to you instead of its owner. → in context
Failed
Net Authority −3 to 0.
Faith X
Gain X Faith: each Faith raises your Public Order track by 1 when resolved.
Fall Back
A controlled retreat that ends the Battle with at least one Retinue left — a partial success.
Fatigue Token
Each token reduces Morale by 2; if your modified Morale is ever 11+, your Army Routs. Tokens stack.
Fatigued
A side at 0 Endurance. Each Skirmish its Field takes a Break check, then it gains a Fatigue Token.
Field
Your Retinues in play — front line (up to 10) plus reserve (up to 5). Casualties leave the field at once, lowering its count.
Florentine
Your Armies may equip two of the same 1H Melee Weapon to gain Dual Wield. While Dual Wielding, your Army gains Parry.
Focused
A natural 10, before modifiers.
Forged
Forged equipment tier. Weapons: Morningstar, Bastard Sword, 2HBastard, War Hammer, Lance; Ranged: Crossbow; Armor: Full Plate; Shield: Tower Shield. Unlocked by: Gilded Foundry (armor & shield), Forge (all).
Garrison
An Army that exists within each of your Settlements while you have the Garrison Infrastructure, sized by that Settlement's Garrison value (Village 10, Town 15, City 25, Metropolis 50 Retinues). Garrisons can't Perform actions or be targeted by actions, and may Sally Forth while their Settlement is under Siege. Garrisons have cost and Supply 0 and don't count toward your Army limit. All your Garrisons share the same Retinue type and equipment, and gain the benefits of all unlocked keywords that apply to your Armies.
Gunpowder
Gunpowder equipment: Arquebus. Unlocked by: Ordnance Yard, Fletchery.
Hamlet
Your farmland Settlement, placed at setup: no Tax Income or Muster Limit, 3 Wards for Natural Holdings only.
Host
Holds the Host Card; the starting player is the player directly clockwise from the Host, including in Spring. The role passes clockwise each Rest Phase (there is no Host in Spring). The Host collects and distributes Trade Income, resolves Bandit Mechanics, and breaks ties (Bandit targeting, and any tie not otherwise resolved).
Immune [keyword]
Can't gain that keyword's effect as it applies to your Army (e.g. Immune Strained, Destroy Shield, Steady, etc.).
Improve
Build Infrastructure, a Wonder included (Industry action). → in context
Influence
The political currency: spend it to Support or Oppose Envoys. You gain it each Empire Phase from your Era, Trade Partners, Alliances, war, Holdings and Infrastructure. Influence +X gives X tokens in the Empire Phase unless it names another time. → in context
Infrastructure
An empire-wide building that applies to every Settlement you have. 5 tiers, Primitive to Wondrous; each needs one active Infrastructure of the tier below.
Initiative
Decides who Strikes first each Skirmish (higher first). Runs −2 to +2 (Ministry can raise the maximum to +3). At −2 or lower you Blunder.
Insolvent
You couldn't cover a cost in full: pay what you can, disband Armies, then set buildings Shuttered. Build Timers halt. It ends once your Treasury is above 0 and you can pay everything you owe.
Intrigue Phase
The Envoy Phase step after the Diplomacy step, before the first Vote. No talking: the starting player uses all of their Intrigue Phase effects, then each player clockwise, ending with the Host. Each choice takes effect at once.
Lay Siege
A Move that ends Adjacent to an at-war Settlement, starting its Siege Timer.
Mastery Chain
A Holding's Mastery Chain is the line of Holdings it builds from, usually running from a Raw Material up to a Monument. You may start building a Holding only while you control one complete line of its Mastery Chain, anywhere in your empire; this is checked only when you start it. A Holding placed in the same Settlement Ward as its immediate parent shares that Ward, and the chain can continue upward the same way. Each Holding carries at most one descendant in its Ward. (Core Principle 13.)
Mastery Chain X
On a Holding tile: placed in the same Settlement Ward as X (the Raw Material or Holding named on it), it shares that Ward instead of taking its own. See Mastery Chain.
Max Influence
The most Influence you may spend to Support or Oppose on one Vote: your Standing Rank in that Domain. Max Influence +X raises it.
Monument
A Domain's capstone Holding, one per game. Having one completes the Monument Edict. → in context
Morale
How steady a Retinue is when tested (lower is steadier; see the Retinue table). Break and Panic checks roll it: a D10 per Retinue in the Field, up to 5 dice, each must meet its modified value; failures are Casualties. If the modified value is ever 11+, the Army Routs.
Move
Advance an Army up to its Speed in Territories (Prowess action). → in context
Muster
Raise a new Army or Reinforce an existing one near a Settlement you Control, while you have a Muster Field; Retinues arrive each Empire Phase over a Muster Timer (Prowess action). → in context
Muster Timer
Turns until a Muster completes; each turn it counts down, the Army gains Retinues up to the combined Muster Limit.
Natural
A Holding property: it pays no Upkeep and may be built in a Hamlet.
Natural roll
The number on the die before any modifiers. Modifiers never change what counts as 'natural'.
Negate [keyword]
Your opponent can't gain that keyword's effect as it applies to their Army (e.g. Negate Shielded).
Net Authority
An Envoy's starting Influence (your Standing Rank) plus every Support, Oppose and Authority X. It sets the outcome: −4 or less Condemned, −3 to 0 Failed (gain Doubt 1), 1 to 3 Passed, 4+ Endorsed. Each Domain's result per outcome is in the Envoy Outcome table.
Next to
Range 2.
Nimble
Gain Initiative +1 in the first Skirmish of each Battle.
One-Shot
Must be Equipped in the first Skirmish of a Battle, and can't be Equipped after it. Requires a Skirmish Field.
Oppose X
Spend X Influence to decrease an Envoy's Net Authority.
Outlaw Country
Uncontrollable Territory zones where Bandit Camps spawn (3 Territories each at Setup). A zone is your Outlaw Country while any of its Territories is within your Province. Foster Rebellion expands it when there is no room; otherwise, when there is no room, its Bandit Camps grow instead. → in context
Panic check
Taken at most once per Skirmish by a side that suffered more than 5 Casualties in that Skirmish (Healed Strikes count, unless Enduring), after both sides have Struck. Roll Morale (5 dice); Immune Panic auto-passes.
Parry
If your Army has gained Parry, roll a D10 to attempt to Parry a Strike before an Armor Save. On an 8+ (better with Improve Parry), the Strike is Parried and has no further effect.
Passed
Net Authority 1 to 3.
Perform
When you Perform an action, immediately do the description of the action. Pay the action's normal Cost.
Personal Envoy
An Envoy you send in the Envoy Phase. How many you get, and how many actions each Performs, rise with the Era.
Pillage
A Prowess action: while your Army stays within Reach of an at-war Settlement, it Extorts half of that Settlement's Holding income each Empire Phase, until it Battles, Lays Siege or Marches. → in context
Planishing
A Focused Save succeeds, regardless of AP.
Poison
When the Defender receives a Strike and rolls a Focused Save, it fails; the resulting wound may only be Healed with a Focused Heal.
Presence
Territory within Range 2 of a non-allied Army (Bandit Armies included, Bandit Camps not), or of a Settlement while its owner has a Garrison (and of its Settlement Wards while the owner has a Keep). An Army can't move within a non-allied Presence unless that Move Performs a Battle against it; a Battle or Lay Siege against a Settlement may pass through the Presence of that Settlement and its Wards.
Province
Every Territory your Settlements' Reach covers, Contested or not. → in context
Public Order
A track from −10 to 10, adjusted each turn by Faith minus Doubt; its band applies cumulative effects (see the Public Order table). → in context
Holding
A building you Build into a Settlement Ward, or into its Mastery Chain parent's Ward. Its effect applies while it's active.
Rally
Auto-passes the FIRST Break check it is ever required to take in a battle (no roll, no casualties, no Rout); every Break check after is normal. One free crisis, then mortal. The only one of the four that keeps shaking a live dial — so it's the one that synergizes with lowering KT base shaking and stacking Abbey +1.
Range X
The amount of Territories you must move in order to get from your current position to the specific Territory.
Ranged Weapons
Ranged weapons: Hunting Bow (Crude), Longbow (Cast), Throwing Axe (Wrought), Crossbow (Forged), Throwing Spear (Tempered), Arquebus (Gunpowder). Unlocked by: Fletchery.
Reach X
How far a Settlement controls, set by its tier: every Territory within Range X of it is in your Province. → in context
Realm
The total collection of Regions.
Recoup X
Undo up to X of the cost you paid (refund gold, or remove Doubt gained). → in context
Heal
If your Army has gained Heal and a to-Save roll fails, roll a D10 & compare it to your Heal value: a result greater than or equal to your Heal value recovers the Retinue. Healed Strikes still count toward the Panic check threshold.
Region
A collection of Territories of the same Type. → in context
Renown
The shared progress track. Gain 1 per Rest Phase; thresholds raise the Era (Ascension 8, Eminence 18, Zenith 30). → in context
Repair
Restore a Damaged Holding or Infrastructure, or Demolish it (Industry action). → in context
Repair Timer
Turns until a Damaged piece is repaired and its effects return.
Resolve
Read an Envoy's Net Authority on the Envoy Resolution table and carry out the result.
Rest Phase
The end of the turn: discard unused Envoys and Influence, advance the Season, score Renown and a Domain Point, pass the Host.
Retinue
A single unit within an Army, represented by a single die roll for Strikes, Saves, etc.
Riposte
If your Army has gained Riposte and rolls a Focused Parry against a Melee Weapon's Strike, you Riposte: your opponent immediately takes a Strike from your melee weapon. You can Riposte a Riposte.
Root
A Holding with no Mastery Chain requirement: you may start it without controlling a parent. If it names a Mastery Chain parent, it may still share that Holding's Settlement Ward.
Rout
The Army breaks and leaves the Battle. Apply Casualties equal to the remaining Retinues. Whenever an Army's modified Morale value reaches 11+, it Routs automatically.
Sack
When a Siege resolves in your favor: Damage the top Holding of each Ward, Extort 2000 per Settlement tier, reduce it one tier and remove one Ward.
Sack Timer
Turns until the sacking Army may Move or Lay Siege again.
Sally Forth
While a Settlement you control is besieged and you have an Army or Garrison inside it, you may Sally Forth: Battle in the Battle Phase without performing an action.
Save
The defender's roll to avoid a Casualty: roll a D10, add the weapon's AP (a negative) and the shield's Save bonus (a positive); the hit is saved on a result >= the armor value.
Season
Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall: one per turn, each with its own effect; the Rest Phase advances it.
Seize the Initiative
Initiative +1 in the first Skirmish. The player who performed the Battle action usually has it; Terrain and the Ministry of Military Strategy can change that.
Serrated
A cumulative −2 penalty to the defender's Heal roll.
Settle
Found a new Village (Industry action). → in context
Settlement
A Village, Town, City or Metropolis (or your Hamlet). It earns Tax Income each Winter, holds Holdings in its Wards, has a Muster Limit, and controls Territory by its Reach.
Settlement Ward
A slot in a Settlement that holds one Holding; a Settlement has one Ward per tier (a Hamlet has 3 Wards, Natural Holdings only). Each Ward occupies a Territory within Range 1 of the Settlement.
Shielded
A cumulative −1 penalty to the Strike roll (to a maximum of 10+). Sources: a shield's −1 to Strike.
Shuttered
A Holding or Infrastructure set inactive while you're Insolvent because its Upkeep went unpaid. It gives no effect and pays no Upkeep, but its Upkeep still counts toward what you owe. It's restored at any Income & Upkeep step where you can pay its Upkeep. If it becomes Damaged, it's no longer Shuttered.
Siege
An Army holding Adjacent to an at-war Settlement while a Siege Timer counts down to Capture or Sack it.
Siege Timer
Turns until a Siege resolves; it doesn't count down in Winter.
Skirmish
One round of a Battle, run through the numbered Battle steps; a Battle repeats Skirmishes until it ends.
Speed X
An Army's movement in Territories per Move action. Base Speed is 5. → in context
Standing
How far you've risen in a Domain, set by its value: Untested, Rising (3+), Established (6+), Sovereign (10+). Each has a Standing Rank and its own Domain effects. → in context
Standing Rank
Untested 1, Rising 2, Established 3, Sovereign 4. Your Envoys in a Domain start at Authority equal to your Standing Rank there; on each Vote in that Domain you may Support or Oppose up to your Standing Rank (your Max Influence).
Steady
Initiative cannot be reduced by Tactics.
Strained
Initiative −1 every Skirmish (negated by Immune Strained). Does not gain Endurance in the Empire Phase.
Strike
A landed hit. Roll a D10, apply modifiers to the roll, and Strike on a result >= the to-Strike number. The target may then Parry, Save, and Heal.
Supply
Gold you pay each Empire Phase for your Armies: each Army's cost by Retinue type, minus every Supply −X (never below 0). Holdings and Infrastructure pay Upkeep instead.
Support X
Spend X Influence to increase an Envoy's Net Authority.
Suzerain
The player who has Vassalized another; the Vassal mirrors their Treaties.
Tactic
A choice both players make secretly and reveal together each Skirmish; it can shift Initiative, Strike, and Save rolls.
Tally Phase
The Envoy Phase step after every Envoy has been voted on: Supports, then the Votes of players who vote in the Tally Phase, then Endorsements, then effects that send another Envoy (voted at once). Resolve begins when it ends.
Targeting you
An effect or action targeting you also targets what you own: your Settlements, Armies and Holdings.
Tempered
Tempered equipment tier. Weapons: Estoc, Poleaxe; Ranged: Throwing Spear; Armor: Gothic Plate; Shield: Heater Shield. Unlocked by: Court Armoury (armor & shield), Advanced Blast Furnace (weapons).
Territory
1 Hex. → in context
Trade Agreement
A Treaty that lets two players trade. While both can trade, each gets Trade Income each turn either of them is Host. → in context
Trade Partner
A player you have a Trade Agreement with and can trade with.
Treasury
The gold you Control.
Treaty
A standing agreement between players (Trade Agreement, Non-Aggression Pact, Alliance), signed in the Diplomacy step. → in context
Truce Timer
Turns until a Truce ends; until then neither player may Declare War on the other or End a Treaty with them.
Unbreakable
Skips the Break check entirely while Fatigued: never rolls, never takes break casualties. BUT still Routs if the morale target climbs to ROUT_THR+ from accumulated Fatigue. Stands fully immune, then collapses all at once — and because it stays full-size up to the Rout, it loses MORE soldiers when it finally breaks than a unit that bled down gradually.
Uncontested
Territory that is Controlled by a single Player's Settlement(s).
Uncontrolled
Territory that is not within Reach of any Player Settlement.
Unshakable
Caps the morale target at CAP_THR permanently. Still TAKES every Break and Panic check each Skirmish (keeps bleeding casualties at a CAP_THR+ roll), but the target can never reach ROUT_THR, so it NEVER Routs. Bends and bleeds forever, never shatters. Makes the shaking stat (and the Abbey bonus) irrelevant — it's capped regardless.
Unstoppable
−2 to the defender's Parry roll (to a maximum of 10+); a Parry against it can't Riposte.
Unwieldy
Initiative cannot be improved by Tactics.
Upkeep
Gold you pay each Empire Phase for your Holdings and Infrastructure. Holding Upkeep by Standing: Untested 100 (Build Timer 0), Rising 200 (Build Timer 1), Established 300 (Build Timer 2), Sovereign 400 (Build Timer 3); Natural 0; each Infrastructure lists its own. Nothing is paid while a piece is inactive (being built, Damaged, under Repair or Shuttered). Upkeep −X lowers the total, never below 0.
Vassal
A player subordinated via Vassalization: their chartered Settlements return to them, they mirror the Suzerain's Treaties, count as allied to the Suzerain, and cannot Perform or be targeted by Diplomacy actions.
Vote
On each Envoy, every player except the sender, in clockwise order from the starting player, must Support, Oppose, or Abstain. No talking during a vote.
War Weariness
Doubt 1 each Empire Phase per consecutive Battle you've lost.
Wild Outlaw Country
Outlaw Country the Host places outside every Province at Setup. After Setup it works like any other Outlaw Country zone.
Within
Range 0.
Wonder
Wondrous-tier Infrastructure, one per game. Several players may build the same Wonder; the first to finish takes it. → in context
Wrought
Wrought equipment tier. Weapons: Arming Sword, Pike, Flail, Halberd, Battle Axe, Cavalry Spear; Ranged: Throwing Axe; Armor: Chainmail; Shield: Kite Shield. Unlocked by: Blacksmith (weapons), Armory (armor & shield).
Zealot
Locks the morale target at base shaking, ignoring ALL modifiers: Fatigue tokens don't raise it, shake bonuses (Abbey) don't lower it. Takes checks every Skirmish at that fixed number, never escalates, never Routs. A fixed wall whose strength is entirely its base stat. Unlike Unshakable (caps at 6), Zealot pins at base — which can be better or worse than 6 — and deliberately ignores Abbey synergy.
to-Strike number
The D10 result a Retinue needs to Strike (see the Retinue table; lower is better). Bonuses add to the roll; penalties and Fatigue Tokens subtract.
