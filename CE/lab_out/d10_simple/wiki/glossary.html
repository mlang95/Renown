Glossary

2H
Cannot use a Shield: while a 2H weapon is Equipped, your Shield gives nothing that Skirmish (no Save bonus, Initiative or keywords).
AP
Armor Penetration — a weapon's (negative) modifier to the defender's Save roll; the more negative, the harder to save.
Abstain
Decline to spend Influence on a vote.
Adjacent
Range 1.
Alliance
A Treaty binding players to mutual support; Military and Defensive Alliances unlock by Era. → in context
At War
Cannot trade; can be the target of Siege, Battle, & Pillage actions from players At War with this player.
Attacker / Defender
The Attacker performed the Move action that caused the Battle or Lay Siege; the other side is the Defender.
Authority X
An automatic +X (or −X) to an Envoy's Authority from a Holding, Infrastructure, or Heritage, applied while the Envoy is sent and resolved; a modifier that depends on the action or its target applies once those are chosen. Authority only matters while an Envoy is being sent and resolved.
Awkward
While equipped with this weapon during a skirmish, you cannot Parry, and so cannot Riposte.
Bandit
Neutral hostile force; Bandit Camps spawn in Outlaw Country and on low Public Order. → in context
Battle
A collection of Skirmishes between two players, continuing until a side's Retinue count is reduced to 0, Routs, or successfully Falls Back, ending the Battle.
Blocked
Initiative −1 in the first Skirmish (negated by Immune Blocked). Armies with Blocked cannot perform Move actions.
Blunder
At Initiative −2, your to-Strike is set to 10+, before other negative modifiers.
Border
Your Province is within or adjacent to another player's Province
Break check
Taken by each Fatigued side's field every Skirmish, just before it gains its Fatigue token. Roll Morale (5 dice, modified by Fatigue tokens); failures are casualties, but a Break check never triggers a Panic check. Unbreakable auto-passes.
Build
Build a Holding, spending its purchase cost and a Settlement ward (Industry action). → in context
Build Timer
Turns until an infrastructure/build completes and becomes Active.
Can Trade
You may take part in trade without active Dirt Roads (you still need a signed Trade Agreement).
Capture Timer
Turns until a Captured Settlement becomes Controlled by the Player with the Capture Timer and applies the listed capture effects.
Cast
Cast equipment tier. Weapons: Daggers, Short Sword, Spear; Ranged: Longbow; Armor: Leather; Shield: Targe Shield. Unlocked by: Tannery (armor & shield), Furnace (weapons).
Cast Armor
Cast-tier armor: Leather. Unlocked by: Tannery.
Cast Shields
Cast-tier shields: Targe Shield. Unlocked by: Tannery.
Cast Weapons
Cast-tier weapons: Daggers, Short Sword, Spear, Longbow. Unlocked by: Furnace.
Casualty
A retinue removed from the field — from an unsaved Strike or a failed Panic or Break check.
Cleave
On a Focused Strike: roll one extra Strike die at your modified to-Strike.
Condemned
Net Authority −3 or less.
Contested
Territory that is Controlled by more than one player's settlement(s).
Controlled
Territory that is within Reach of a Player's Settlement(s).
Convert Timer
Turns until a Convert attempt resolves (or fails early if conditions change).
Cost / Pay
Cost to perform an action, usually in Gold or Doubt.
Council Envoy
A free Envoy resolved in the Council Phase on the voted Domain; auto-Abstained, net Authority floored at 1.
Council Phase
Before Personal Envoys, a brief Forum, then all players vote on a Domain with no talking (clockwise; Host breaks ties). Each then sends a free Council Envoy of that Domain. Council Envoys auto-Abstain and their net Authority cannot drop below 1.
Craft
Craft Holdings count toward how much income your Trade Agreements generate — any effect that grants Craft +X. For each active Trade Agreement, both players gain 100 × the Host's Craft X.
Crude
Crude equipment tier. Weapons: Farm Tool, Cudgel, Pitchfork; Ranged: Hunting Bow; Armor: Padded; Shield: Buckler Shield. Unlocked by: Animal Husbandry (armor & shield).
Crude Armor
Crude-tier armor: Padded. Unlocked by: Animal Husbandry.
Crude Shields
Crude-tier shields: Buckler Shield. Unlocked by: Animal Husbandry.
Crude Weapons
Crude-tier weapons: Farm Tool, Cudgel, Pitchfork, Hunting Bow.
Deadly
On a Focused Strike: that strike's AP is increased by 5, and the defender may Parry or Heal only with a Focused roll.
Demand Tribute
Coerce gold or concessions from another player (Prowess). → in context
Destroy Shield
On a Focused Strike: the target loses its Shield attributes for the rest of the Battle.
Diplomacy
Free Envoy actions for forming and ending Treaties and Trade Agreements; resolved before Domain Envoys.
Domain
One of the four identities — Industry, Prowess, Cunning, Piety — raised by spending Domain points. Values 3/6/10 = Rising/Established/Sovereign Standing. → in context
Domain Point
Gain 1 each Rest Phase; spend to raise a Domain value by 1.
Doubt X
Gain X Doubt: each Doubt lowers your Public Order track by 1 when resolved.
Drilled
Does not lose Endurance in the first Skirmish of each Battle.
Dual Wield
A failed Strike is rerolled once; the rerolled Strike can be Focused. Dual Wield confers Two-Handed. You cannot reroll successful Strikes.
Dual-equip
Carry two weapons at once (e.g. melee + ranged). Granted by the Tiltyard.
Edict
A scoring achievement. Grants the Realm +1 Renown every time one is achieved. → in context
Endorsed
Net Authority 3+.
Endurance
A side's stamina. Each side that fights loses 1 per Skirmish; at 0 it becomes Fatigued.
Enduring
Successfully Healed Strikes don't count toward the Panic check threshold.
Envoy
The currency of actions: send an Envoy to perform an action during the Envoy Phase. Council Envoys act on the voted Domain; Personal Envoys are sent by Era progression.
Envoy Outcome
How a Sent Envoy resolves, by Net Authority: Condemned (<= −3), Failed (−3 < Net <= 0, gain Doubt 1), Passed (>= 1), Endorsed (>= 3). The per-domain effect at each band is given by the Envoy Outcome Table; an action's own endorsed bonus overrides the domain default.
Equipped
The weapon chosen for the Skirmish.
Expand
Raise a Settlement one tier: Village to Town, Town to City, City to Metropolis (Industry action). → in context
Extort X
Take X from the stated source: the gold goes to you instead of its owner. → in context
Fail
An unsuccessful combat roll or Envoy resolution.
Failed
Net Authority 0, −1, or −2.
Faith X
Gain X Faith: each Faith raises your Public Order track by 1 when resolved.
Fall Back
A controlled retreat that ends the Battle with at least one retinue left — a partial success.
Fatigue Token
Each token reduces Morale by 2; if your modified Morale is ever 11+, your army Routs. Tokens stack.
Fatigued
A side at 0 Endurance. Each Skirmish its field takes a Break check, then it gains a Fatigue token.
Field
Your retinues in play — front line (up to 10) plus reserve (up to 5). Casualties leave the field at once, lowering its count.
Focused
A natural 10, before modifiers.
Forged
Forged equipment tier. Weapons: Morningstar, Bastard Sword, 2HBastard, War Hammer, Lance; Ranged: Crossbow; Armor: Full Plate; Shield: Tower Shield. Unlocked by: Gilded Foundry (armor & shield), Forge (all).
Forged Armor
Forged-tier armor: Full Plate. Unlocked by: Gilded Foundry, Forge.
Forged Shields
Forged-tier shields: Tower Shield. Unlocked by: Gilded Foundry, Forge.
Forged Weapons
Forged-tier weapons: Morningstar, Bastard Sword, 2HBastard, War Hammer, Lance, Crossbow. Unlocked by: Forge.
Garrison
An Army that exists within a Settlement that contains the Garrison Infrastructure, according to its Garrison value (Village 10, Town 15, City 25, Metropolis 50 Retinues). Garrisons can't perform actions or be targeted by actions, and may Sally Forth while their Settlement is under Siege. Garrisons have cost and upkeep 0 and don't count toward your Army limit. All Garrisons within an Empire share the same Retinue type and equipment, and gain the benefits of all unlocked keywords that apply to your Armies.
Gunpowder
Gunpowder equipment: Arquebus. Unlocked by: Ordnance Yard, Fletchery.
Host
Holds the Host Card; the starting player is the player directly clockwise from the Host, including in Spring. The role passes clockwise each Rest Phase (there is no Host in Spring). The Host collects and distributes Trade Income, resolves Bandit Mechanics, and breaks ties (Council vote, bandit targeting, and any tie not otherwise resolved).
Immune [keyword]
Can't gain that keyword's effect as it applies to your Army (e.g. Immune Strain, Destroy Shield, Steady, etc.).
Improve
Construct Infrastructure (Industry action). → in context
Influence
The political currency of voting. Spend it to Support or Oppose Envoys. You gain it each Empire Phase from your Era, innate modifiers (trade partners, alliances, war), Holdings, and Infrastructure. An effect reading Influence +X gives you X Influence tokens in the Empire Phase, unless it names another time. → in context
Initiative
Decides who Strikes first each Skirmish (higher first). Runs −2 to +2 (Ministry can raise the maximum to +3). At −2 or lower you Blunder.
Intrigue Phase
The Envoy Phase step after the Forum, before the first vote. No talking: the starting player uses all of their Intrigue Phase effects, then each player clockwise, ending with the Host. Each choice takes effect at once.
Mastery Chain
A Holding's Mastery Chain is the line of Holdings it builds from, usually running from a Raw Material up to a Monument. You may start building a Holding only while you control one complete line of its Mastery Chain, anywhere in your empire; this is checked only when you start it. A Holding placed in the same Settlement Ward as its immediate parent shares that Ward, and the chain can continue upward the same way. Each Holding carries at most one descendant in its Ward. (Core Principle 13.)
Mastery Chain X
While this Holding occupies the same Settlement Ward as X (the Raw Material or Holding named on its tile), it uses no ward of its own — the two share one ward. Placed anywhere else, it fills a ward normally. (Core Principle 13.) Note: Two Holdings that chain from the same Holding cannot share a Ward with each other.
Max Influence
The most Influence you may spend to Support or Oppose on one vote: your Standing rank in that Domain (the Era's rank on Diplomacy Envoys). Max Influence +X raises it.
Monument
A Domain's capstone Holding. Completing one scores its Edict and grants a powerful effect. → in context
Morale
How steady a retinue is when tested (lower is steadier; see the retinue table). Break and Panic checks roll it: a D10 per retinue in the field, up to 5 dice, each must meet its modified value; failures are casualties. If the modified value is ever 11+, the army Routs.
Move
Advance an Army up to its Speed in Territories (Prowess). → in context
Muster
Raise a new Army or Reinforce an existing one near a Muster Field; Retinues arrive each Empire Phase over a Muster Timer (Prowess action). → in context
Muster Timer
Turns remaining in a Muster; each turn it counts down, the Army gains Retinues up to the combined muster limit.
Natural
Natural Holdings do not cost Upkeep & can be used in Hamlets.
Natural roll
The number on the die before any modifiers. Modifiers never change what counts as 'natural'.
Negate [keyword]
Your opponent can't gain that keyword's effect as it applies to their Army (e.g. Negate Shielded).
Net Authority
The sum of an Envoy's starting Influence (1+ by Standing) and all Support, Oppose, and Authority X. The total sets the outcome: −3 or less Condemned, 0 or less Failed (gain Doubt +1), 1+ passes.
Next to
Range 2.
Nimble
Gain Initiative +1 in the first Skirmish of each Battle.
One-Shot
Must be Equipped in the first Skirmish of a Battle, and can't be Equipped after it. Requires a Tiltyard.
Oppose X
Spend X Influence to decrease an Envoy's net Authority.
Outlaw Country
Uncontrollable Territory zones where Bandit Camps spawn (3 Territories each at Setup). A zone is your Outlaw Country while any of its Territories is within your Province. Foster Rebellion expands it when there is no room; otherwise, when there is no room, its Bandit Camps grow instead. → in context
Panic check
Taken at most once per Skirmish by a side that suffered more than 5 casualties in that Skirmish (Healed Strikes count, unless Enduring), after both sides have Struck. Roll Morale (5 dice); Immune Panic auto-passes.
Parry
If your Army has gained Parry, roll a D10 to attempt to Parry a Strike before an Armor Save. On a 8+ (Improved by Improved Parry), the Strike is Parried and has no further effect.
Passed
Net Authority 1 or 2.
Perform
When you Perform an action, immediately do the description of the action. Pay the action's normal Cost.
Personal Envoy
An Envoy you send in the Envoy Phase to attempt to perform an action; the amount of Envoys you can spend and the volume of actions you can perform with one scales with Era.
Planishing
A Focused Save succeeds, regardless of AP.
Poison
When the Defender receives a Strike and rolls a Focused Save, it fails; the resulting wound may only be Healed with a Focused Heal.
Presence
Territory within Range 2 of a Settlement or Army (Bandit Armies included, Bandit Camps not). An Army can't move within the Presence of a non-allied Settlement or Army unless that Move performs a Battle or Lay Siege against it.
Province
The collection of Contested Territory and Controlled Territory by your Settlement's Reach. → in context
Public Order
A track from −10 to 10, adjusted each turn by Faith minus Doubt; its band applies cumulative effects (see the Public Order table). → in context
Holding
An object that is placed into a Settlement Ward or onto a Root Holding as a result of a Build action, giving effects which affect your Empire in many ways.
Rally
Auto-passes the FIRST Break check it is ever required to take in a battle (no roll, no casualties, no Rout); every Break check after is normal. One free crisis, then mortal. The only one of the four that keeps shaking a live dial — so it's the one that synergizes with lowering KT base shaking and stacking Abbey +1.
Range X
The amount of Territories you must move in order to get from your current position to the specific Territory.
Ranged Weapons
Ranged weapons: Hunting Bow (Crude), Longbow (Cast), Throwing Axe (Wrought), Crossbow (Forged), Throwing Spear (Tempered), Arquebus (Gunpowder). Unlocked by: Fletchery.
Reach
How far a Settlement projects control, in Territories (by tier). Calculated like Range X → in context
Reach X
The Range X characteristic which determines what Territories you Control and are in your Province. → in context
Realm
The total collection of Regions.
Recoup X
Undo up to X of the cost you paid (refund gold, or remove Doubt gained). → in context
Heal
If your Army has gained Heal and a to-Save roll fails, roll a D10 & compare it to your Heal value: a result greater than or equal to your Heal value recovers the retinue. Healed Strikes still count toward the Panic check threshold.
Region
A collection of Territories of the same Type. → in context
Renown
The shared progress track. Gain 1 per Rest Phase; thresholds raise your Era (Ascension 8, Eminence 18, Zenith 30). → in context
Repair
Restore damaged Infrastructure or Settlements (Industry). → in context
Repair Timer
Turns until a Damaged piece is repaired and its effects return.
Resolve
Determine modified Authority and follow Envoy Resolution Table.
Retinue
A single unit within an Army, represented by a single die roll for Strikes, Saves, etc.
Riposte
If your Army has gained Riposte and rolls a Focused Parry against a Melee Weapon's Strike, you Riposte: your opponent immediately takes a Strike from your melee weapon. You can Riposte a Riposte.
Root
A Holding with no Mastery Chain requirement: you may start it without controlling a parent. If it is Efficient with a Holding, it may still share that Holding's Settlement Ward.
Rout
The army breaks and leaves the Battle. Apply casualties equal to the remaining retinues. Whenever an army's modified Morale value reaches 11+ or more, it Routs automatically.
Sack Timer
Cooldown before the same force may Lay Siege again (per Sack rules).
Sally Forth
While a settlement you control is besieged and you have an army or Garrison inside it, you may Sally Forth: Battle in the Battle Phase without performing an action.
Save
The defender's roll to avoid a casualty: roll a D10, add the weapon's AP (a negative) and the shield's Save bonus (a positive); the hit is saved on a result >= the armor value.
Seize the Initiative
Typically, the player who performed the Battle Action gains Seize the Initiative, but Terrain and the Ministry of Military Strategy can affect that. When a player gains Seize the Initiative, the player gains Initiative +1 in the first skirmish.
Send an Envoy
Declare a Domain, spend 1 Envoy.
Serrated
A cumulative −2 penalty to the defender's Heal roll.
Settle
Found a new Village (Industry action). → in context
Settlement Ward
A slot in a Settlement that holds one Holding; a Settlement has one Ward per tier (a Hamlet has 3 Wards, Natural Holdings only). Each Ward occupies a Territory within Range 1 of the Settlement.
Shielded
A cumulative −1 penalty to the Strike roll (to a maximum of 10+). Sources: a shield's −1 to Strike.
Siege
Sieging a Settlement with an Army to capture it; Siege Timers don't count down in Winter.
Siege Timer
Turns remaining until a Siege resolves.
Skirmish
One round of a Battle, run through the numbered Battle steps; a Battle repeats Skirmishes until it ends.
Speed X
An Army's movement range in Territories per Move action. Base Speed value of an army is Speed 5 → in context
Standing
Your tier in a Domain: Untested, Rising (3), Established (6), Sovereign (10). Its rank sets your Envoys' starting Authority and how much you may Support or Oppose per vote in that Domain, and it unlocks Domain effects. → in context
Standing Rank
Untested 1, Rising 2, Established 3, Sovereign 4. Your Envoys in a Domain start at Authority equal to your rank there; on each vote in that Domain you may Support or Oppose up to your rank (your Max Influence). Diplomacy uses the Era's rank.
Steady
Initiative cannot be reduced by Tactics.
Strained
Initiative −1 every Skirmish (negated by Immune Strain). Does not gain Endurance in the Empire Phase.
Strike
A landed hit. Roll a D10, apply modifiers to the roll, and Strike on a result >= the to-Strike number. The target may then Parry, Save, and Heal.
Support X
Spend X Influence to increase an Envoy's net Authority.
Suzerain
The player who has Vassalized another; the vassal mirrors their Treaties.
Tactic
A choice both players make secretly and reveal together each Skirmish; it can shift Initiative, Strike, and Save rolls.
Tally Phase
The Envoy Phase step after every Envoy has been voted on: Supports, then the votes of players who vote in the Tally Phase, then Endorsements, then effects that send another Envoy (voted at once). Resolve begins when it ends.
Targeting you
An effect or action targeting you also targets what you own: your Settlements, Armies and Holdings.
Tempered
Tempered equipment tier. Weapons: Estoc, Poleaxe; Ranged: Throwing Spear; Armor: Gothic Plate; Shield: Heater Shield.
Tempered Armor
Tempered-tier armor: Gothic Plate.
Tempered Shields
Tempered-tier shields: Heater Shield.
Tempered Weapons
Tempered-tier weapons: Estoc, Poleaxe, Throwing Spear.
Territory
1 Hex. → in context
Treaty
A standing agreement between players (e.g. Trade Agreement, Non-Aggression, Alliance) signed via Diplomacy. → in context
Truce Timer
Turns remaining until a Truce expires (and related diplomacy restrictions end).
Unbreakable
Skips the Break check entirely while Fatigued: never rolls, never takes break casualties. BUT still Routs if the morale target climbs to ROUT_THR+ from accumulated Fatigue. Stands fully immune, then collapses all at once — and because it stays full-size up to the Rout, it loses MORE soldiers when it finally breaks than a unit that bled down gradually.
Uncontested
Territory that is Controlled by a single Player's Settlement(s).
Uncontrolled
Territory that is not within Reach of any Player Settlement.
Unshakable
Caps the morale target at CAP_THR permanently. Still TAKES every Break and Panic check each Skirmish (keeps bleeding casualties at a CAP_THR+ roll), but the target can never reach ROUT_THR, so it NEVER Routs. Bends and bleeds forever, never shatters. Makes the shaking stat (and the Abbey bonus) irrelevant — it's capped regardless.
Unstoppable
−2 to the defender's Parry roll (to a maximum of 10+); a Parry against it can't Riposte.
Unwieldy
Initiative cannot be improved by Tactics.
Upkeep
Gold you pay each Empire Phase for Armies, Holdings and Infrastructure. Holding upkeep is by Standing: Untested 100 (Build Timer 1), Rising 200 (Build Timer 2), Established 300 (Build Timer 3), Sovereign 400 (Build Timer 4); Natural 0; a Holding pays no upkeep while its Build Timer is running. Army upkeep = the sum of each Army's cost (by Retinue type) − Upkeep modifiers.
Vassal
A player subordinated via Vassalization: their chartered settlements return to them, they mirror the Suzerain's Treaties, count as allied to the Suzerain, and cannot perform or be targeted by Diplomacy actions.
Vote
On each Envoy, every player except the sender, in clockwise order from the starting player, must Support, Oppose, or Abstain. No talking during a vote.
War Weariness
gain Doubt 1 for each consecutive Battle you lose in the Empire Phase.
Wild Outlaw Country
Outlaw Country the Host places outside every Province at Setup. After Setup it works like any other Outlaw Country zone.
Within
Range 0.
Wrought
Wrought equipment tier. Weapons: Arming Sword, Pike, Flail, Halberd, Battle Axe, Cavalry Spear; Ranged: Throwing Axe; Armor: Chainmail; Shield: Kite Shield. Unlocked by: Blacksmith (weapons), Armory (armor & shield).
Wrought Armor
Wrought-tier armor: Chainmail. Unlocked by: Armory.
Wrought Shields
Wrought-tier shields: Kite Shield. Unlocked by: Armory.
Wrought Weapons
Wrought-tier weapons: Arming Sword, Pike, Flail, Halberd, Battle Axe, Cavalry Spear, Throwing Axe. Unlocked by: Blacksmith.
Zealot
Locks the morale target at base shaking, ignoring ALL modifiers: Fatigue tokens don't raise it, shake bonuses (Abbey) don't lower it. Takes checks every Skirmish at that fixed number, never escalates, never Routs. A fixed wall whose strength is entirely its base stat. Unlike Unshakable (caps at 6), Zealot pins at base — which can be better or worse than 6 — and deliberately ignores Abbey synergy.
to-Strike number
The D10 result a retinue needs to Strike (see the retinue table; lower is better). Bonuses add to the roll; penalties and Fatigue tokens subtract.
