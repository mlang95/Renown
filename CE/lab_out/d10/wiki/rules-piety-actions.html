Piety Actions

ActionCostRequiresEffect (if passes)Endorsed
Spread GospelDoubt 1—Each non-allied player gains Doubt 1. Each allied player gains Faith 1.Gain Faith 1.
Send MissionariesDoubt 1—Choose a target player or Alliance. If the target is outside your Alliance, each player in it gains Doubt 2. If the target is inside your Alliance, each other allied player gains Faith 2.Gain Faith 1.
TitheDoubt 1—Choose a player. Extort 10% of their Treasury (round down to the nearest 100, minimum 0).Gain Faith 1.
ConvertDoubt 1—Choose another player's closest non-capital Settlement; its Public Order must be −5 or lower. Lay Siege using only Settlement-type Siege modifiers (Settlement Size and Citadel) and set a Convert Timer. When it reaches 0, the Settlement joins your empire (see Capture). — If the target's Public Order rises to 1 or higher before the timer reaches 0, the Convert fails and the timer is removed.Gain Faith 1.
CrusadeDoubt 1Sovereign PietyDeclare War on a non-ally you have no NAP or truce with, then immediately perform a Move action with one of your Armies. — While a Crusade is active, neither player may Declare War on, Sign or End a Treaty with, Negotiate with, or Demand Tribute from the other.; You may have only one active Crusade at a time. It ends only when one of the two players is Vassalized or otherwise removed from the game.Gain Faith 1.
