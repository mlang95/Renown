Actions 19

Every Envoy action, grouped by Domain. Each is Sent during the Envoy Phase, voted on, and resolved on a pass.

Prowess

ActionCostRequiresEffect (if passes)Endorsed
MoveNone—Move an Army up to its Speed in Territories. Then choose one: March — move up to 2× Speed, lose 1 Endurance, take no other action; Battle — end adjacent to a non-allied Army not in a Settlement, then begin a Battle; Lay Siege — end adjacent to an at-war Settlement, then begin a Siege; Muster — end within range 2 of your Settlements, not within range 1 of a non-ally, at an active Muster Field, then recruit up to your combined muster limit (you may swap Retinues between adjacent allied Armies and change equipment).<br><span class='mut'>An Army may be the target of only one Move action per turn.; While an Army&#x27;s Battle, Siege, or Muster Timer is running, it can&#x27;t be the target of actions.; When an Army performs the Battle mode, it has Seize the Initiative in that Battle&#x27;s first Skirmish.</span>Perform another Move action (same or a different Army).
Declare WarNoneRising ProwessChoose a non-allied player you have no NAP or active truce with. You and that player are now At War, and any Trade Agreements between you end. If the target is in a Defensive Alliance, every member is now At War with you.<br><span class='mut'>If you are in a Military Alliance, then at the start of your next turn every member Declares War on the target and sends 1 fewer Envoy that turn.</span>Perform a Move action.
Demand TributeNone—Choose a non-allied player you have no NAP with, then negotiate terms. If the target agrees, sign a NAP or Alliance (the target picks among the offered terms). If the target refuses, or later fails to meet the terms, immediately Declare War on them.<br><span class='mut'>Terms may include gold, Settlements, Territory, Treaties, or promises. Industry

ActionCostRequiresEffect (if passes)Endorsed
Build2000 gold—Choose an unlocked, available Infrastructure and set a Build Timer equal to its build time. When it completes, the Infrastructure becomes active in every Settlement in your province.<br><span class='mut'>You must have at least one Infrastructure from the prior tier before building the next tier.; A Wonder is built in your capital and requires all other Infrastructure to be active when the Build action is performed.</span>Recoup 2000.

Charter2000 gold—Choose one: Charter a new Village — on uncontrolled or in-province non-water, non-mountain Territory, range 4+ from any Settlement and range 2+ from Outlaw Country; or Expand a non-City Settlement one tier into an adjacent Territory (Village to Sea/Town, Sea/Town to Port/City, Port/City to Metropolis), adding a Ward.<br><span class='mut'>To upgrade to City or Metropolis you must meet that tier&#x27;s requirements; otherwise set Build Timer 1.</span>Recoup 2000.
