Battle Phase

Begin Battle

Begin Skirmish

  • Declare Army — declare which Army will Skirmish (if more than one is adjacent to either Army in the Battle).
  • Choose a Tactic — place a face-down Tactic; reveal once the other player has too.
  • Declare equipment (if you have a tiltyard or a one-shot weapon).
  • Resolve Tactic modifiers — after Tactics are revealed, apply the Initiative and any other modifiers from your Tactics and weapon profile.

How a Battle Resolves

A Battle is fought as a series of Skirmishes, run through the steps below until one side is wiped out, Routs, or successfully Falls Back. Full keyword detail is in the Compendium glossary.

Begin the Battle — Seize the Initiative

Determine who gains Seize the Initiative. Typically, the player who performed the Battle Action gains Seize the Initiative, but Terrain and the Ministry of Military Strategy can affect that. When a player gains Seize the Initiative, the player gains +1 Initiative in the first round of combat.

The Skirmish Steps

  • Form the line. Each side places up to 10 Retinues in its front line — one Strike die each — and may keep up to 5 in reserve to replace losses as they fall. An Army of 25 keeps the remainder in camp; between Skirmishes, camp and reserves refill the front line and reserve to their maximums.
  • Choose Tactics. Both players secretly pick one Tactic, then reveal together.
  • Declare equipment. The Attacker names equipment first; the Defender responds.
  • Initiative. Initiative ranges from −2 to +2. The higher Initiative Strikes first this Skirmish. At −2 or lower you Blunder — you can Strike only on a natural 10.
  • Roll to Strike. Roll a D10 for each front-line Retinue, applying its modifiers. It Strikes on a result ≥ its to-Strike number (Levy 7+, Man-at-Arms 5+, Sergeant 3+, Knight Templar 4+). A natural 10 may trigger Cleave, Deadly, or Destroy Shield.
  • Strike and defend. Resolve the first side's Strikes — the defender may Parry (D10, 8+ cancels; a natural 10 is a Riposte), then Save (D10 + the weapon's AP + the shield's bonus ≥ the armor value), then Recover (after a failed Save, a final D10 ≥ the Recover value). Unsaved, unrecovered Strikes are casualties, and leave the field at once.
  • Panic check. If the side about to Strike back took 5 or more casualties this Skirmish, it first takes a Panic check: roll its Morale, up to 5 dice; failures are casualties. A check ever modified to 11 or more Routs the whole Army.
  • Strike back. The other side Strikes the same way, if able, taking any Panic check its own casualties trigger.
  • Lose Endurance. Each side that fought loses 1 Endurance. A side at 0 Endurance is Fatigued.
  • Break check. Before gaining its token, each Fatigued side's field takes a Break check: roll a D10 per Retinue in the field, up to 5 dice, each ≥ its modified Morale value; failures are casualties. A Break check never triggers a Panic check. A check ever modified to 11 or more Routs the whole Army.
  • Fatigue token. Each Fatigued side then gains a Fatigue token — each token is −2 to that Army's Morale rolls; Tokens stack and last until the Battle ends.
  • End the Skirmish. The Battle ends if a side is wiped out, Routs (modified Morale 11+), or successfully Falls Back. Otherwise refill the lines and begin the next Skirmish.

Resolve Battle

  • Remove all Fatigue tokens.
  • The player who won extorts spoils of war.

Spoils of War: extort the total cost of the Retinues, by type, you destroyed in the Battle.

End Battle
